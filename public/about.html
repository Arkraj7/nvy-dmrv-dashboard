About Urban Forest Park — Institutional Governance & Editorial Verification

Urban Forest Park is an independent public-interest registry for climate, forestry, ESG, geospatial and academic careers in India, and the home of the Nagar Van dMRV Dashboard and Green Calculator. We publish vacancies only after source, deadline and application-link checks, and we never accept payment for inclusion.

Our mission

India's climate transition depends on people being able to find credible, timely opportunities in forestry, conservation, environmental science, sustainability, geospatial analysis and academic research. Urban Forest Park exists to make that search transparent. Every active and archived job is presented with its organisation, state, position type, deadline and a direct link to the official application page — no application fees and no sponsored ranking.

Editorial & research verification methodology

Our workflow combines automated source monitoring with human review. The standard below applies to every registry record, including historical archive entries and international opportunities.

  1. 1. Source discovery

    We monitor official government portals, university and institute recruitment pages, public-sector notices, verified feeds and institutional announcements. A record is never created from an unattributed social-media post or a third-party job aggregator alone.

  2. 2. Normalisation & duplicate control

    Title, organisation, location, position type, deadline, compensation and application URL are normalised into a consistent schema. Duplicate notices, expired windows and records without a resolvable official application link are rejected.

  3. 3. Human editorial review

    The editorial lead checks the source page, confirms the application destination and reviews the summary for accuracy and tone. Confidential, undisclosed and unknown employers are blocked from publication by policy. Listings that cannot be verified are held back rather than published speculatively.

  4. 4. Publication, monitoring & correction

    Active jobs are refreshed daily. Expired jobs move to the public archive rather than disappearing. Corrections can be submitted through the issue-reporting form; we aim to acknowledge substantive corrections within two business days.

Domain expertise behind the registry

Environmental & earth sciences

Editorial review is grounded in environmental and sustainable development training, with attention to ecology, climate policy and evidence quality.

Geospatial dMRV & remote sensing

The Nagar Van dashboard and Green Calculator translate satellite, field and survey data into readable urban-forest indicators, with clear source notes and limitations.

Urban forestry & heat-island mitigation

We prioritise jobs and research that connect plantation, maintenance and community outcomes to measurable cooling, carbon and biodiversity benefits.

Environmental law & public policy

Policy and legal context is reviewed against Indian environmental governance, including Nagar Van Yojana, CAMPA and national climate commitments.

Vision for a Viksit Bharat

India's Nationally Determined Contributions require decentralised, data-driven action. Nagar Vans and Nagar Vatikas can create carbon sinks, reduce the urban heat-island effect and strengthen community resilience. This platform is built so that a park visitor, a researcher and a policymaker can all use the same transparent evidence base.

Governance disclosures

Funding & independence

Listing is free. No employer, consultancy or institution pays for inclusion, ranking or editorial coverage. Hosting and maintenance costs are met independently of listing fees.

Data sources & limitations

Registry records are derived from official recruitment pages and public notices. The dMRV dashboard additionally uses government records, field surveys and satellite-derived indicators. We link to primary sources, but users should verify critical eligibility, dates and compensation before applying.

Update cadence

The active registry, archive, pathway hubs and sitemap are refreshed by the daily publishing pipeline. The homepage and hub pages display the latest editorial review date.
