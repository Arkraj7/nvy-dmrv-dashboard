Accessibility Statement

Urban Forest Park is committed to ensuring that the National Climate, Forestry, ESG & Geospatial Career Registry, its dispatches and its public research utilities are usable by the widest possible audience — including candidates, researchers and public servants who rely on assistive technology. This Statement describes the Platform's accessibility commitments, the features implemented, known limitations, and the channel through which barriers can be reported.

Section 1.0 Commitment & Scope

1.1 This Statement covers the public web properties operated at www.urbanforestpark.com, including the career registry, the five career-pathway hub pages, the legal and compliance documents, the subscription and consent interfaces, and the informational pages of the dMRV Dashboard and Green Calculator.

1.2 Accessibility is treated as a continuing obligation rather than a one-time audit. The Platform reviews interface changes for accessibility impact and welcomes reports of barriers from any user.

Section 2.0 Standards Conformance

2.1 The Platform targets conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), and aligns its practices with the accessibility expectations of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 and applicable Government of India guidelines for public digital services.

2.2 The four conformance principles applied are:

  • Perceivable — content is presented with sufficient contrast, resizeable text and multiple structural cues, not by colour alone;
  • Operable — all interactive functions are reachable and operable by keyboard, with visible focus indication;
  • Understandable — documents use plain, formal language; interface behaviour is predictable and consistent across pages;
  • Robust — pages are built with semantic HTML and ARIA attributes where necessary, compatible with contemporary assistive technologies.

Section 3.0 Accessibility Features

3.1 The following features are implemented across the public surface:

  • Light / Night mode — a persisted high-contrast interface scheme is available from the header of every page, honouring the operating-system preference on first visit;
  • Keyboard navigation — skip-to-content links, logical tab order, focus-visible outlines, and Escape-key dismissal for overlays;
  • Semantic document structure — landmark regions, hierarchical headings, and clause-level anchor navigation in legal documents;
  • Text legibility — relative font sizing that respects browser zoom up to 200% without loss of content, and formal serif typography in statutory documents for sustained reading;
  • Reduced-motion support — animation is suppressed where the operating system requests reduced motion;
  • Accessible forms — explicit labels, native validation semantics, helper text and status announcements for the subscription and consent interfaces;
  • Consistent navigation — the top navigation, theme control and document navigation behave identically across every legal and informational page.

Section 4.0 Known Limitations & Remediation

4.1 Certain embedded visualisations within the research utilities (dashboard charts, spatial viewers) are rendered by canvas- or SVG-based components whose screen-reader descriptions are provided at a narrative level rather than at full data-point granularity. Underlying datasets remain available as structured files and can be requested in alternative formats through the contact channel below.

4.2 Third-party content — including advertisements served by Google AdSense and externally hosted application portals — is outside the Platform's direct control. Where a third-party component is reported to present an accessibility barrier, the Platform will evaluate an accessible alternative or removal.

4.3 Where any content cannot be made accessible in its present form, the Platform will provide the information in an alternative format upon request, ordinarily within 10 working days.

Section 5.0 Feedback & Contact

5.1 Users who encounter an accessibility barrier, or who require content in an alternative format, are invited to contact the Platform. Reports should identify the page and describe the barrier encountered; a response will ordinarily follow within 7 working days.
