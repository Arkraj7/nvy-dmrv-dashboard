What the sector involves

Renewable energy is India's largest green employer. Roles span project development, engineering, operations and maintenance, policy and finance.

  • A bachelor's in electrical, mechanical or energy engineering is the standard entry for technical roles.
  • Solar PV design, wind resource assessment and grid integration are the most listed technical skills.
  • Site and field experience, even as an intern, is valued more than a second certificate.
  • State renewable energy agencies, private developers and EPC contractors are the main employers.
  • India's renewable target of 500 GW by 2030 means sustained hiring for the next decade.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is renewable energy a good career in India?
Yes. India has one of the world's largest renewable targets and the sector hires engineers, project managers and analysts at scale.
Do I need a master's degree?
Not for most plant and design roles. Research and R&D posts often prefer one.
Are government jobs available?
Yes. State renewable energy agencies and public sector units recruit engineers regularly.
What is the pay like?
Entry-level engineers start at 4-8 LPA. Project managers and senior engineers earn 12-25 LPA. Pay varies by company size and location.
