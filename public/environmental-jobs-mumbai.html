Mumbai hiring snapshot

Mumbai's environmental market spans finance, consulting and coastal regulation. ESG teams at banks and consultancies, the Maharashtra Pollution Control Board and research groups such as WRI India and TERI generate openings across the spectrum.

What the work looks like

Roles in Mumbai range from field ecology and forestry to environmental impact assessment, pollution monitoring, GIS and sustainability reporting. Field posts usually ask for a master's in environmental science, forestry, ecology or a related discipline; consulting and corporate roles add compliance, data and reporting skills.
