Section 3.0 Accuracy of Automated & AI-Structured Job Summaries

3.1 Registry entries are compiled through an automated harvest-and-verification pipeline. Source notices are normalised, de-duplicated and structured — in part with the assistance of machine-based (AI) summarisation — into standardised dossiers covering position title, organisation, location, qualification, compensation or fellowship reference, and the application window.

3.2 While the Platform applies domain verification, redirect-unwrapping, duplicate hashing and editorial structuring to every entry, automated summaries may contain condensation errors, interpolation, or may lag behind subsequent corrigenda issued by the publishing institution. Stipend figures, eligibility criteria, reservation provisions, deadlines and extensions are frequently revised after first publication.

3.3 Candidates are advised — and it is a condition of use of the registry — to always verify deadlines and official notifications directly at the linked parent institution website before acting. The authoritative source for every mandate is the official notification or portal of the concerned institution; the registry entry is a discovery aid and not a substitute for it.

Verification Obligation

Every dossier provides both the direct application link and the verified official website of the parent organisation. Users must treat the official notification as controlling in the event of any discrepancy with the condensed registry summary.
