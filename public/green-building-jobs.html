What the sector involves

Green building specialists work on certification (LEED, GRIHA, IGBC), energy efficiency and sustainable construction. Real estate developers and consulting firms hire.

  • A degree in architecture, civil engineering or environmental engineering is standard.
  • LEED or GRIHA accreditation is listed in most postings.
  • Energy modelling and building simulation skills are valued.
  • Consulting firms and real estate developers are the main employers.
  • India is among the top countries for LEED-certified space, creating sustained demand.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is LEED?
Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design — a global green building certification by USGBC.
What is GRIHA?
Green Rating for Integrated Habitat Assessment — India's national green building rating system.
Is green building a growing field?
Yes. India is among the top countries for LEED-certified space and IGBC membership is growing.
Do I need an architecture degree?
For design roles, yes. For certification and energy audit roles, engineering backgrounds are accepted.
