Frequently Asked Questions

Which government bodies hire in forestry and wildlife in India?
State forest departments, the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), ICFRE institutes, the Wildlife Institute of India, state biodiversity and wildlife boards, SACON and national parks authorities regularly advertise research, project and contractual positions. The registry tracks these official portals daily.
What is a Young Professional role in the environment sector?
Young Professional (YP) positions are contractual scheme roles — commonly in the 0–3 year experience band — with ministries, forest departments and research institutes. They typically run for one to three years, may be renewable, and follow institute or scheme pay norms.
Are these permanent government jobs?
The registry covers both permanent recruitments and contractual or project-mode engagements. Each listing states the nature of the role, the application window and the official portal where the recruitment is administered — always verify details in the original notification.
How do I apply for forest department vacancies?
Open the position dossier and follow the official application link — forest department and institute recruitments are administered through their own portals, walk-in interviews or email applications as stated in the advertisement. The registry never charges fees for listings.
What field roles are included beyond forest guard recruitment?
The hub covers forest ranger and forester jobs, wildlife biologist and wildlife conservation roles, ecology jobs and conservation jobs, and environmental officer posts. It also lists RFO, ACF and IFS recruitment and research positions.
