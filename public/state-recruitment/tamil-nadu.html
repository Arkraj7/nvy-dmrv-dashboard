Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a single exam for Tamil Nadu?
No. Each body runs its own recruitment. State PSCs conduct gazetted posts; departments conduct field posts.
How do I stay updated?
Check the portals listed above weekly during recruitment season. The registry lists vacancies as they are published.
Are these government jobs?
Most are state government posts. Some are contractual project positions funded by central schemes.
