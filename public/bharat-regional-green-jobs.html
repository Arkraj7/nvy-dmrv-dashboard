Bharat Regional Portal — Frequently Asked Questions

How are state-wise green jobs grouped on this portal?
The regional portal groups active jobs into language and cultural regions used by India's environmental employers: the Hindi belt, Marathi-speaking Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Telugu states, Karnataka, West Bengal and Gujarat. Each state link opens the live registry pre-filtered to that state, with the same verified records and official application links as the main registry.
Do regional links open the live registry or a separate database?
They open the single national registry with a state filter applied. The regional portal is a crawlable entry point, not a duplicate dataset, so deadlines, eligibility and application links always match the active registry.
Are the regional listings bilingual?
Every verified job carries an English title and summary, and the main registry includes an EN | हिंदी switch. The Hindi portal and this regional portal provide additional language-native entry points for candidates who prefer to browse in Hindi or another Indian language.
