This page aggregates observed compensation for PhD / Doctoral Fellowship roles across 23 verified green-jobs listings in India. Figures are taken verbatim from official notifications and may include stipends, fellowships, CTC or consolidated pay. Use them to benchmark your expectations before applying; always confirm the latest amount on the employer's official page.

How to read these numbers

  • Not disclosed: the employer did not publish a figure; ask during the interview or check the official notification.
  • Stipend / fellowship: usually monthly, for project staff and research fellows.
  • CTC / annual: full-time corporate or NGO roles, often with variable pay and benefits.
  • LPA: lakhs per annum (1 LPA = Rs 1,00,000 per year).
