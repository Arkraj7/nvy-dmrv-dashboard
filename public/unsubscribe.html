Dispatch Subscription Preferences

This page administers subscriptions to the Mandate Dispatch — the email briefing of verified climate, forestry, ESG and academic mandates issued every 72 hours. Your right to withdraw consent and opt out is guaranteed under Section 5.0 of the Privacy Policy.

Section 1.0 Unsubscribe from the Dispatch

1.1 Enter the email address that receives the dispatch below. Your unsubscribe request will be composed as a formal communication to the Grievance Officer; you may send it directly from your email client. You may also simply reply to any dispatch email with the word "unsubscribe".

Email address on the subscription
Action
Compose Unsubscribe Request

Alternatively: use the unsubscribe link appended to the footer of every dispatch email, or write directly to arkraj.biswas6@gmail.com with the subject "Unsubscribe — Mandate Dispatch".

Section 2.0 What Happens After Your Request

2.1 On receipt of a valid unsubscribe request, the Platform will:

  • remove the address from the active dispatch list within 7 working days;
  • erase the subscription record, including the stored email address and preferences, within 30 days, in accordance with the retention limits in the Privacy Policy;
  • send one final confirmation that the removal has been effected.

2.2 Withdrawal is prospective: you will not receive dispatches issued after removal, while bulletins already issued remain unaffected. Unsubscribing from the dispatch does not affect access to the registry, the archive, or the Telegram and WhatsApp alert channels.

Section 3.0 Resubscribe & Questions

3.1 You may resubscribe at any time from the dispatch section on the homepage registry. Resubscription constitutes fresh consent under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

3.2 Questions about this page, or difficulties completing an opt-out, may be addressed to the Grievance & Data Protection Officer at arkraj.biswas6@gmail.com.
