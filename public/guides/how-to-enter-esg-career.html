How to Enter an ESG Career in India

A practical guide for graduates and professionals who want to move into ESG and sustainability roles in India.

Updated
03 Nov 2026

Step by step

  1. Understand what ESG roles involve. Read the ESG analyst profile and the ESG glossary entry. ESG work ranges from data collection to strategy.
  2. Build foundational knowledge. Learn BRSR, GRI and the GHG Protocol. Free resources are available on SEBI's and WRI's websites.
  3. Get relevant experience. Internships at consulting firms, ESG rating agencies or corporate sustainability teams are the standard entry path.
  4. Build a portfolio. Create a mock BRSR section, a carbon inventory or a data dashboard. Show it in interviews.
  5. Apply for entry-level roles. Browse ESG and sustainability jobs and internships. Entry-level titles include ESG analyst, sustainability associate and ESG data analyst.
  6. Consider certifications. GRI, SASB and BRSR training courses exist. They help but are not required for most entry-level roles.
  7. Network. LinkedIn, sustainability conferences and industry events are where ESG professionals connect.

Practical tips

  • ESG hiring in India is dominated by consulting firms and large listed companies. Target these.
  • Data skills (Excel, Power BI, Python) are increasingly listed in ESG job descriptions.
  • A master's in environment, economics or business helps but is not mandatory for analyst roles.
  • ESG roles rarely publish pay. Ask about CTC breakup, variable component and assurance budget during interviews.
  • The field is growing fast. Early movers with 2-3 years of experience are in high demand.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a science degree for ESG?
No. Economics, business, law and social science backgrounds are common in ESG roles.
Is ESG a stable career?
Yes. BRSR mandates and investor pressure are structural drivers, not trends.
How much do ESG analysts earn?
The registry shows that most ESG listings do not publish pay. See the <a href='/roles/esg-analyst'>ESG analyst profile</a> for details.
