Forest Guard Exam Preparation Guide

A comprehensive preparation guide for state forest guard recruitment exams.

Updated
03 Nov 2026

Written exam preparation

  • General awareness: state geography, forests, wildlife, national parks, environmental schemes. This is the highest-weightage section.
  • Arithmetic: basic operations, percentages, ratios, simple interest. School-level difficulty.
  • Reasoning: analogies, series, coding-decoding, blood relations. Practice from previous papers.
  • Language: state language paper. Focus on grammar, comprehension and vocabulary.
  • Forestry basics: forest types, tree species, wildlife, conservation. State-specific topics.

Physical test preparation

  • Running: most states require 1600m-25km in a specified time. Start running 3-4 months before.
  • Walking: some states have walking tests instead of running. Check the notification.
  • Height and chest: measurements are taken. Know the standards for your category.
  • Build stamina gradually. Do not overtrain. Consistency matters more than intensity.

Documents checklist

  • School leaving certificate (Class 10/12)
  • Caste certificate (if applicable)
  • Domicile certificate
  • Physical fitness certificate from a government doctor
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Application form (as per state format)
  • Identity proof (Aadhaar, voter ID)

Timeline

  1. 3-4 months before: Start physical preparation. Begin studying general awareness and arithmetic.
  2. 2 months before: Solve previous years' papers. Focus on weak areas.
  3. 1 month before: Revise. Take mock tests. Check physical standards.
  4. 1 week before: Rest. Verify documents. Check exam centre and reporting time.
  5. Exam day: Carry all documents. Arrive early. Stay calm.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many vacancies are there usually?
It varies by state and year. Some states recruit hundreds; others recruit a few dozen. Check the notification.
Is coaching necessary?
No. Self-study with previous papers and basic fitness preparation is sufficient for most candidates.
Can I apply in multiple states?
Yes. Each state has its own recruitment. You can apply to any state where you meet the eligibility.
