GIS Interview Questions and How to Answer Them

A preparation guide for GIS and remote sensing job interviews in India.

Updated
03 Nov 2026

Technical questions

  • What is the difference between vector and raster data? Vector: points, lines, polygons. Raster: grid of pixels. Each has different use cases.
  • What is a coordinate system vs projection? Coordinate system defines location. Projection is how the 3D earth is flattened to 2D.
  • What is NDVI? Normalized Difference Vegetation Index — measures vegetation health from satellite imagery.
  • What is Google Earth Engine? A cloud platform for planetary-scale geospatial analysis. Used for land cover, deforestation monitoring, etc.
  • What is the difference between supervised and unsupervised classification? Supervised uses training samples. Unsupervised groups pixels by similarity.

Software questions

  • QGIS vs ArcGIS? QGIS is free and open-source. ArcGIS is proprietary but industry standard. Know both.
  • What Python libraries do you use for GIS? GeoPandas, Rasterio, Shapely, Folium, PyQGIS.
  • How do you handle large raster datasets? Tiling, pyramids, cloud-optimized GeoTIFFs, chunked processing.
  • What is PostGIS? Spatial database extension for PostgreSQL. Used for spatial queries and storage.

Case study questions

  • How would you map deforestation in a region? Satellite imagery (Landsat/Sentinel), NDVI change detection, classification, accuracy assessment.
  • How would you create a suitability map? Multi-criteria analysis: identify factors, assign weights, overlay layers, reclassify.
  • How would you validate your results? Ground truthing, accuracy assessment (confusion matrix), cross-validation.

How to prepare

  1. Practice with real data. Download free satellite imagery and build a small project.
  2. Learn QGIS thoroughly. It is free and widely used in Indian institutes.
  3. Learn Google Earth Engine. Free, powerful, and increasingly listed in job postings.
  4. Build a portfolio. 2-3 maps with documented methodology. Show them in interviews.
  5. Know your projections. UTM, WGS84, and India-specific projections (Everest, WGS84).

Frequently Asked Questions

How technical are GIS interviews?
Very technical. Expect live software tests, data manipulation exercises and conceptual questions.
Do I need a GIS degree?
No. Science and engineering graduates with demonstrated GIS skills are hired regularly.
