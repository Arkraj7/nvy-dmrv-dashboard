How to Prepare a BRSR Report

A practical guide to preparing SEBI's Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report, from data collection to disclosure.

Updated
07 Nov 2026

Step by step

  1. Understand the framework. BRSR has two sections: essential indicators (mandatory) and leadership indicators (voluntary). Download the format from SEBI's website.
  2. Identify data owners. Map each indicator to the department that holds the data: HR for employee metrics, facilities for energy and water, procurement for supply chain.
  3. Collect data for the full financial year. BRSR covers the entire fiscal year, not a snapshot. Set up data collection processes early.
  4. Calculate emissions. Use the GHG Protocol to compute Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions. Document methodology and emission factors used.
  5. Draft the report. Follow the SEBI format exactly. Use clear language and avoid vague claims without data.
  6. Get internal review. Have legal, finance and sustainability teams review before submission.
  7. Consider assurance. SEBI is phasing in third-party assurance. Check the current circular for your company's phase.
  8. File with the annual report. BRSR is part of the annual report filed with stock exchanges.

Practical tips

  • Start data collection in Q1, not after year-end. Retroactive data collection is the most common failure.
  • BRSR is mandatory for the top 1000 listed companies by market capitalisation. Smaller companies file voluntarily.
  • The format changes periodically. Always use the latest SEBI circular.
  • Assurance requirements are being phased in by company size. Check which phase applies to you.
  • ESG analyst and sustainability reporting specialist roles are the main jobs created by BRSR.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BRSR mandatory for all companies?
No. It applies to the top 1000 listed companies by market capitalisation, phased in from FY2022-23.
How long does BRSR preparation take?
For a first-time filer, 3-6 months including data collection and review.
Can I outsource BRSR preparation?
Yes. Consulting firms and ESG specialists offer BRSR services, but internal ownership is recommended.
