India's Green Job Market in 2026: Trends, Salaries & Where to Apply

Published 29 October 2026 · By Arkraj Biswas, Urban Forest Park Editorial

India's green economy is accelerating. With SEBI's BRSR mandate, India's net-zero 2070 commitment, and a surge in climate-tech investment, the demand for sustainability professionals has never been higher. The Urban Forest Park registry currently tracks 139+ verified openings across 57 career pathways — and that number grows daily.

1. Market overview: what's driving green hiring

  • Regulatory push: SEBI's BRSR (Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting) is now mandatory for the top 1,000 listed companies. CSRD and IFRS S1/S2 are pushing Indian multinationals toward global disclosure standards.
  • Corporate net-zero targets: Over 100 Indian companies have announced net-zero commitments, creating demand for carbon accounting, decarbonization strategy and ESG reporting specialists.
  • Government programmes: Nagar Van Yojana, CAMPA, Green India Mission and state-level climate action plans are driving forestry, watershed and conservation hiring.
  • Climate tech investment: India's climate-tech startups raised over $2.5 billion in 2025, creating roles in renewable energy, carbon markets, sustainable agriculture and green hydrogen.
  • Global supply chain pressure: EU's CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism) and corporate Scope 3 mandates are forcing Indian exporters to measure and reduce supply-chain emissions.

4. In-demand skills for 2026

  • Carbon accounting: GHG Protocol (Scope 1, 2, 3), ISO 14064, PCAF for financed emissions.
  • ESG reporting: BRSR, GRI, CDP, SASB, TCFD, IFRS S1/S2, CSRD/ESRS.
  • Data skills: SQL, Python, R, Power BI/Tableau — increasingly required for emissions data management.
  • GIS/remote sensing: QGIS, ArcGIS, Google Earth Engine, satellite imagery analysis.
  • Climate risk: TCFD scenario analysis, physical and transition risk assessment.
  • Supply chain: Scope 3 supplier engagement, CBAM compliance, sustainable procurement.

6. Tips for job seekers

  1. Build a portfolio: Do a pro-bono ESG assessment or carbon audit for a small business or NGO.
  2. Get certified: GRI, CDP or SASB FSA certification signals credibility to employers.
  3. Network actively: Join LinkedIn groups, attend climate events (TERI WSDS, CSE workshops, UN conferences).
  4. Tailor applications: Highlight specific framework knowledge and data skills in your resume.
  5. Set up alerts: Join our Telegram channel for daily job alerts.

Frequently asked questions

Is the green job market growing in India?

Yes. India's green economy is projected to create 3.5 million new jobs by 2027. SEBI's BRSR mandate, India's net-zero 2070 commitment and climate-tech investment are the primary drivers.

Can freshers get green jobs?

Yes. Entry-level roles exist in ESG data analysis, field research, GIS mapping and sustainability reporting. Our ESG Jobs for Freshers hub lists verified entry-level openings.

What is the average salary for a green job in India?

Entry-level roles pay ₹3–7 LPA, mid-level ₹10–18 LPA, senior ₹25–45 LPA and leadership roles ₹50–100+ LPA. Salaries vary by sector, city and specialization.
