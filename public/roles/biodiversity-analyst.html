In short

A biodiversity and natural capital analyst assesses ecosystem services and nature-related risks. A master's in ecology or environmental science is common; TNFD is creating new demand.

How people enter

Drawn from the qualification and experience requirements in the current listings.

  • A master's in ecology, biodiversity, environmental science or economics is common.
  • TNFD and nature-based solutions (NbS) frameworks are the emerging standards.
  • Field survey skills and species identification are valued for conservation roles.
  • Think tanks, NGOs and consultancies are the main employers.

How to benchmark when nothing is published

Most Indian notices leave pay out. These questions get you a comparable answer without a guessed band.

  • Ask for the fixed and variable split. A headline CTC that is 20% variable is not the same as a fixed package.
  • Ask what the band is for the grade. Companies that will not quote a number will usually confirm a band once you are shortlisted.
  • Compare the whole offer. Provident fund, gratuity, insurance, travel and field allowance change the effective value materially in this sector.
  • Check the contract type. Fellowship, project contract and permanent posts are taxed and renewed differently; the notice states which one applies.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TNFD?
Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures — a framework for reporting nature-related risks.
Is this a new field?
Yes. Nature-related disclosure is emerging, similar to where climate disclosure was 5 years ago.
Do I need field experience?
For conservation roles, yes. For corporate natural capital roles, data and finance skills matter more.
