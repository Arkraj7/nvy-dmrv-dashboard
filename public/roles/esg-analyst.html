ESG Analyst Salary in India: Pay, Skills & Entry Route

ESG analysts collect and structure environmental, social and governance data for disclosure, ratings and assurance. The work is part reporting, part data quality control and part explaining numbers to people who do not work in sustainability.

In short

An ESG analyst collects and reports environmental, social and governance data under frameworks like BRSR and GRI. Entry usually needs a master's or strong internship; pay is rarely published in Indian notices.

How people enter

Drawn from the qualification and experience requirements in the current listings.

  • A master's in environmental science, economics, business or sustainability helps, but several listings accept a bachelor's with relevant internship experience.
  • Learn the reporting frameworks actually named in Indian notices: BRSR, GRI, and increasingly CSRD for global teams.
  • Build one artefact you can show: a mock BRSR section, a carbon inventory, or a data dashboard.
  • Expect a screening test or case exercise on data interpretation before the interview.

How to benchmark when nothing is published

Most Indian notices leave pay out. These questions get you a comparable answer without a guessed band.

  • Ask for the fixed and variable split. A headline CTC that is 20% variable is not the same as a fixed package.
  • Ask what the band is for the grade. Companies that will not quote a number will usually confirm a band once you are shortlisted.
  • Compare the whole offer. Provident fund, gratuity, insurance, travel and field allowance change the effective value materially in this sector.
  • Check the contract type. Fellowship, project contract and permanent posts are taxed and renewed differently; the notice states which one applies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do so few ESG listings publish pay?
Corporate and consulting roles in India often negotiate pay privately, and the notice states only the role. The table above shows every listing that did publish a figure, plus the count that did not.
Is ESG analyst a good first job?
It is one of the few green roles that hires at entry level in volume. The trade-off is that much of the first year is data collection and documentation rather than strategy.
Do I need a CA or CFA?
No. Assurance and valuation teams value them, but most analyst postings ask for sustainability, environment or economics backgrounds.
