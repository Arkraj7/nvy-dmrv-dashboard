In short

A carbon analyst builds greenhouse-gas inventories and models reduction pathways using the GHG Protocol. Entry needs quantitative skills and sector knowledge.

How people enter

Drawn from the qualification and experience requirements in the current listings.

  • Learn the GHG Protocol scopes properly; most interview questions start there.
  • Sector knowledge matters: cement, steel, power and logistics have different abatement options.
  • Spreadsheet and data modelling skills are assumed; Python or Power BI is increasingly listed.
  • A master's in environmental engineering, energy or economics is common but not universal.

How to benchmark when nothing is published

Most Indian notices leave pay out. These questions get you a comparable answer without a guessed band.

  • Ask for the fixed and variable split. A headline CTC that is 20% variable is not the same as a fixed package.
  • Ask what the band is for the grade. Companies that will not quote a number will usually confirm a band once you are shortlisted.
  • Compare the whole offer. Provident fund, gratuity, insurance, travel and field allowance change the effective value materially in this sector.
  • Check the contract type. Fellowship, project contract and permanent posts are taxed and renewed differently; the notice states which one applies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is carbon accounting the same as carbon trading?
No. Accounting measures and reports emissions. Trading buys and sells credits or allowances. Some roles cover both, most do not.
Which certification helps?
Employers name frameworks (GHG Protocol, SBTi, ISO 14064) more often than certificates. Learn the framework and show a worked inventory.
Is the role quantitative?
Yes. Expect emission-factor calculations, unit conversions and scenario modelling in the first interview.
