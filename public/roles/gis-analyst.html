In short

A GIS analyst turns spatial data into maps and models using QGIS, ArcGIS and Google Earth Engine. Entry accepts science or engineering graduates with demonstrated GIS skills.

How people enter

Drawn from the qualification and experience requirements in the current listings.

  • QGIS or ArcGIS plus one scripting language (Python or R) is the standard minimum.
  • Google Earth Engine appears in a growing share of environmental listings.
  • A portfolio of two or three maps with documented methods is more persuasive than a certificate list.
  • Degrees range from geography and environmental science to civil engineering and geoinformatics.

How to benchmark when nothing is published

Most Indian notices leave pay out. These questions get you a comparable answer without a guessed band.

  • Ask for the fixed and variable split. A headline CTC that is 20% variable is not the same as a fixed package.
  • Ask what the band is for the grade. Companies that will not quote a number will usually confirm a band once you are shortlisted.
  • Compare the whole offer. Provident fund, gratuity, insurance, travel and field allowance change the effective value materially in this sector.
  • Check the contract type. Fellowship, project contract and permanent posts are taxed and renewed differently; the notice states which one applies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which software should I learn first?
QGIS, because it is free and used across institutes, then ArcGIS if the employers you target list it. Add Python for automation.
Is GIS a good entry into environmental work?
Yes. It is one of the thinnest-supply categories in the registry relative to demand, and it accepts science and engineering graduates.
Do I need a geoinformatics degree?
No. Several listings accept any science or engineering degree with demonstrated GIS skills.
