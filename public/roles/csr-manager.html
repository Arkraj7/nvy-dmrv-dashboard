In short

A CSR manager oversees a company's social responsibility spend under Section 135 of the Companies Act. A master's in social work or business is common; programme management experience is listed in most postings.

How people enter

Drawn from the qualification and experience requirements in the current listings.

  • A master's in social work, development studies or business is common.
  • Programme management and NGO partnership experience are listed in most postings.
  • Impact measurement and reporting skills are increasingly required.
  • Large listed companies and their foundations are the main employers.

How to benchmark when nothing is published

Most Indian notices leave pay out. These questions get you a comparable answer without a guessed band.

  • Ask for the fixed and variable split. A headline CTC that is 20% variable is not the same as a fixed package.
  • Ask what the band is for the grade. Companies that will not quote a number will usually confirm a band once you are shortlisted.
  • Compare the whole offer. Provident fund, gratuity, insurance, travel and field allowance change the effective value materially in this sector.
  • Check the contract type. Fellowship, project contract and permanent posts are taxed and renewed differently; the notice states which one applies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CSR the same as ESG?
No. CSR is a spending obligation; ESG is a performance framework. They overlap in practice.
Is CSR mandatory in India?
Yes. Companies meeting profit/turnover/net-worth thresholds must spend 2% of average net profit on CSR activities.
Does CSR pay well?
It varies. Foundation and large company roles pay reasonably; smaller company roles are modest.
