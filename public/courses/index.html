GRI Standards

The most widely used sustainability reporting standard globally. Covers economic, environmental and social impacts.

Global Reporting Initiative Details →

BRSR (Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting)

India's mandatory ESG disclosure framework for the top 1000 listed companies. Covers 90+ indicators across environmental, social and governance.

SEBI / NSE Academy Details →

GHG Protocol

The standard for measuring and managing greenhouse gas emissions. Covers Scope 1, 2 and 3. Foundation of carbon accounting.

World Resources Institute (WRI) Details →

LEED (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design)

Global green building certification. Covers sustainable site development, water efficiency, energy, materials and indoor environment.

US Green Building Council (USGBC) Details →

GRIHA

India's national green building rating system. Covers site planning, water, energy, waste and indoor environment.

GRIHA Council (TERI) Details →

IGBC (Indian Green Building Council)

India's green building rating system by CII. Covers new construction, existing buildings, interiors and landscapes.

CII - IGBC Details →

SBTi (Science Based Targets initiative)

Framework for setting emission reduction targets aligned with climate science. Covers target setting, validation and progress tracking.

SBTi (CDP, WRI, WWF, UN Global Compact) Details →

ISO 14001 (Environmental Management Systems)

International standard for environmental management systems. Covers planning, implementation, monitoring and improvement.

International Organization for Standardization Details →

NEBOSH (National Examination Board in Occupational Safety and Health)

International qualification in occupational health and safety. Covers risk assessment, hazard control and safety management.

NEBOSH Details →

GRI Professional Certification

Professional certification in GRI sustainability reporting. Covers reporting principles, disclosures and assurance.

Global Reporting Initiative Details →

SASB (Sustainability Accounting Standards Board)

Industry-specific sustainability accounting standards. Used by investors for ESG analysis.

SASB (now part of IFRS Foundation) Details →

TCFD (Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures)

Framework for climate-related financial risk disclosure. Covers governance, strategy, risk management and metrics.

Financial Stability Board Details →

TNFD (Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures)

Framework for nature-related financial risk disclosure. Emerging standard, similar to TCFD but for biodiversity.

TNFD Details →

GHG Protocol Corporate Standard

The corporate accounting and reporting standard for greenhouse gas emissions. Foundation of carbon accounting.

WRI / WBCSD Details →

ISO 14064 (Greenhouse Gas Verification)

International standard for greenhouse gas quantification and verification. Used by carbon auditors and verifiers.

International Organization for Standardization Details →

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these certifications mandatory?
No. Most are voluntary. Employers list them as 'preferred' or 'desirable' rather than essential.
Which should I get first?
Start with free frameworks (GHG Protocol, BRSR format, GRI standards). Paid certifications add value once you have a target role.
Do certifications guarantee a job?
No. They signal commitment and knowledge. Practical experience and a portfolio matter more.
