Junior Research Fellow (JRF) - Tiger Ecology & Landscape Connectivity

Wildlife Institute of India (WII) · Dehradun, Uttarakhand

JRF / SRF Uttarakhand Entry Level / Fellowship
Apply on wii.gov.in ↗ Official website ↗

Role overview

The Wildlife Institute of India recruits a Junior Research Fellow for its tiger ecology programme covering corridor connectivity and prey dynamics in the Terai Arc Landscape. The fellow supports camera-trap based monitoring, occupancy analysis and spatial modelling for landscape-level conservation planning. The engagement is project-based, initially for one year and extendable as per funding norms.

Key responsibilities

  • Support camera-trap deployment, retrieval and image processing across assigned forest divisions
  • Run occupancy, abundance and connectivity analyses under the guidance of the project scientist
  • Compile field datasets, maintain spatial databases and prepare analytical maps in QGIS
  • Draft progress notes, data summaries and inputs for scientific publications
  • Coordinate with state forest department staff and field assistants during surveys

Eligibility & qualifications

M.Sc. in Wildlife Science / Ecology / Zoology / Environmental Science (UGC-NET or GATE preferred)

Wildlife survey methodsCamera-trap data analysisQGISR or Python for ecological statisticsOccupancy modellingField ecology.

Compensation / fellowship

Rs 37,000/month + HRA (DST JRF norms); Rs 42,000/month after SRF upgrade
