Role overview

Future Earth is a global research network advancing sustainability science, linking thousands of researchers through programmes such as the Global Land Programme and Sustainability Transitions. Its opportunities desk circulates jobs, fellowships and calls from network members and partner institutions worldwide. India has one of the network's largest research communities, so many listed roles are open to Indian applicants or offer remote participation.

Key responsibilities

  • Track global sustainability-science opportunities and calls
  • Support network research programmes and working groups
  • Assist with events, synthesis products and communications
  • Connect regional communities with global initiatives

Eligibility & qualifications

Varies by listing: research, programme and communications roles in sustainability science typically require a relevant master's degree or equivalent experience.
