ESG, Carbon Markets & Corporate Sustainability

Analyst - Sustainable Sourcing

Avantor · Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu

Full-Time Corporate / NGO Tamil Nadu Mid-Senior (3-6 Years)
Apply on linkedin.com ↗ Official website ↗

Role overview

This role supports the organization’s supply chain decarbonization strategy by monitoring supplier sustainability performance and compliance. Based in Coimbatore, you will manage data collection for emissions tracking and responsible sourcing initiatives. You will work closely with the Sustainable Sourcing Director to prepare reports and maintain supplier scorecards that align with global regulatory requirements.

Key responsibilities

  • Manage supplier engagement and data submissions on platforms like EcoVadis and Kodiak Hub.
  • Analyze supplier performance data to publish regional sustainability scorecards.
  • Support GHG emissions tracking by collecting and organizing data from diverse sources.
  • Monitor updates to sustainability standards and assist with annual regulatory campaigns.
  • Coordinate with internal stakeholders to verify data accuracy and resolve audit non-conformances.

Eligibility & qualifications

Bachelor's / Master's degree in the relevant discipline or equivalent experience

Supply chain decarbonizationGHG emissions trackingSupplier assessment platformsData visualization (Power BI)Sustainability reporting frameworksAdvanced Excel.
