Role overview

The Wildlife Institute of India offers this intensive postgraduate diploma program to build professional capacity in wildlife conservation and management. Students engage with expert faculty in Dehradun to study biodiversity, habitat assessment, and protected area management. This program is designed for forest officers and researchers seeking advanced technical training to address complex conservation challenges across diverse Indian ecosystems.

Experience level: Entry Level / Fellowship

Qualification: Master's degree in the relevant discipline; doctoral registration as per institute / fellowship norms (UGC-NET / GATE preferred)

Key responsibilities include:

  • Attend advanced lectures on wildlife biology and ecology
  • Conduct field-based research in protected areas
  • Analyze data related to habitat management and species conservation
  • Participate in technical workshops and seminars
  • Prepare comprehensive reports on wildlife management strategies
