Role overview

Key responsibilities

  • Development and implementation of the community conservation strategy: Develop and adapt WCS Gabon’s national community conservation strategy to the local context
  • Support the development and implementation of community strategies at the site level
  • Identify priorities for community engagement and promote initiatives that strengthen community support for conservation goals
  • Contribute to the revitalization and operation of the Community-Based Local Management Committees (CCGL)
  • Ensure coordination with the ANPN to guarantee alignment with national guidelines
