Role overview

IICA, an autonomous organisation under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, offers an Advanced Programme on CSR, ESG and Sustainability with faculty-led sessions, exam preparation, real-world assignments, and a capstone project. The cohort starts 1 November 2026 and enrolment closes 31 October 2026.

Experience level: Entry Level / Fellowship

Qualification: Open to CSR, ESG, and sustainability learners and career aspirants, including participants of the SWAYAM CSR, ESG and Sustainability programme.

Key responsibilities include:

  • Complete faculty-led sessions on CSR, ESG, and sustainability
  • Work through case studies and practical assignments
  • Prepare for the SWAYAM CSR, ESG and Sustainability examination
  • Complete a capstone project
