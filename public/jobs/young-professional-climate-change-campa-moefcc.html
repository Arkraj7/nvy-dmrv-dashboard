Young Professional (Climate Change) - CAMPA & Climate Finance

Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) · New Delhi, Delhi NCR

Young Professional / Consultant (Contractual) Delhi NCR 1-3 Years
Apply on moef.gov.in ↗ Official website ↗

Role overview

The Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change engages Young Professionals to support climate change policy work, including CAMPA-linked interventions and domestic climate finance tracking. The role involves inter-ministerial coordination, data analysis for reporting and preparation of policy inputs. The engagement is contractual and based in New Delhi.

Key responsibilities

  • Assist in drafting policy notes, briefing materials and meeting agendas
  • Support data compilation and analysis for climate reporting obligations
  • Track state-level CAMPA activities and compile progress summaries
  • Coordinate with stakeholders across ministries and state departments
  • Maintain files, dashboards and presentation decks for the division

Eligibility & qualifications

Master's degree in Environment / Climate Change / Forestry / Public Policy from a recognised university

Climate policy understandingMS Office and data analysisPolicy draftingGovernment coordinationReport preparation

Compensation / fellowship

Rs 50,000 - Rs 70,000/month consolidated (Young Professional norms)
