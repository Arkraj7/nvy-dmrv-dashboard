Atlas France to Southeast Asia Postdoctoral Mobility Fellowship

Fondation Maison des sciences de l'homme · Remote (India), Multiple States / Remote

Apply on www2.fundsforngos.org ↗ Official website ↗

Role overview

This mobility fellowship supports postdoctoral researchers based in India to conduct research in France or Southeast Asia. The program facilitates international academic exchange for scholars investigating social, environmental, and geographical challenges. Selected fellows will engage with host institutions to advance their research projects while building cross-border professional networks in the humanities and social sciences.

Key responsibilities

  • Conduct independent research in host institutions across France or Southeast Asia
  • Collaborate with international academic partners on interdisciplinary projects
  • Participate in scholarly seminars and academic exchange activities
  • Produce high-quality research outputs and publications
  • Develop professional networks within the global research community

Eligibility & qualifications

PhD in Social Sciences or Humanities with a focus on environmental or geographical research.
