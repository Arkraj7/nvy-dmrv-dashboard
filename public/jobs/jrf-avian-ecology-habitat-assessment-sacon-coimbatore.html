Junior Research Fellow (JRF) - Avian Ecology & Habitat Assessment

Salim Ali Centre for Ornithology and Natural History (SACON) · Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu

JRF / SRF Tamil Nadu Entry Level / Fellowship
Apply on sacon.in ↗ Official website ↗

Role overview

SACON invites applications for a Junior Research Fellow to study avian communities and habitat quality across wetland and woodland landscapes in southern India. The fellow will run point-count and transect surveys, quantify habitat variables and analyse community responses to land-use change. The position is contractual for one year, renewable based on performance and project continuation.

Key responsibilities

  • Conduct bird surveys using point-count and line-transect protocols across study sites
  • Collect habitat and disturbance variables along survey plots
  • Digitise survey layers and produce habitat maps using QGIS
  • Maintain daily field logs and consolidate datasets for analysis
  • Support preparation of project reports and research manuscripts

Eligibility & qualifications

M.Sc. in Zoology / Wildlife Biology / Ecology / Environmental Sciences with bird survey experience

Bird identification and survey protocolsStatistical analysis in RQGISField data collectionScientific report writing.

Rs 37,000/month + HRA (as per funding agency norms)
