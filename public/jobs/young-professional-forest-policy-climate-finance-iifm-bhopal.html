Forestry, Wildlife & Afforestation

Young Professional - Forest Policy & Climate Finance

Indian Institute of Forest Management (IIFM) · Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh

Young Professional / Consultant (Contractual) Madhya Pradesh 1-3 Years
Apply on iifm.ac.in ↗ Official website ↗

Role overview

IIFM Bhopal engages a Young Professional to support research and advisory work on forest policy, climate finance and community forestry institutions. The professional will draft policy notes, support training programmes and assist in evaluating afforestation-linked finance mechanisms. The engagement is contractual, based at the Bhopal campus with occasional travel.

Key responsibilities

  • Draft policy briefs and background papers on forest governance themes
  • Support design and delivery of training programmes for forest officers
  • Assist research teams in data collection and literature synthesis
  • Coordinate with state forest departments and partner organisations
  • Prepare presentations and documentation for institutional partners

Eligibility & qualifications

Master's degree in Forestry / Environmental Management / Economics / Public Policy; strong writing skills
