Role overview

IUCN's Regional Office for South America has been based in Quito, Ecuador since 1991, supported by an office in Brasilia, Brazil, and leads conservation work across the Amazon, the Andes and the páramos - species and ecosystem conservation, protected-area management, landscape restoration, ecosystem-based adaptation to climate change and bioeconomy. It recruits conservation, restoration and programme professionals on a rolling basis, and international candidates, including Indians, apply directly through the official IUCN portal.

Experience level: Mid-Senior (3-6 Years)

Qualification: Degree in conservation biology, environmental science, natural-resource management or a related field, with experience as specified per regional vacancy.

Key responsibilities include:

  • Run species, forest or marine conservation programmes
  • Prepare Red List assessments and biodiversity guidance
  • Work with members, commissions and partner governments
  • Support ecosystem-based adaptation planning across the region

About this opportunity

Forestry and wildlife roles in India are driven by state forest departments, CAMPA-funded afforestation, NGOs and research institutes. Candidates with field experience in forest inventory, wildlife monitoring, GIS and community mobilisation are in high demand.
