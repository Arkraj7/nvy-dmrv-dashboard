United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) · New York / Country Offices (Global), United States

Apply on jobs.undp.org ↗ Official website ↗

Role overview

UNDP's Nature, Climate and Energy practice supports countries on climate action, biodiversity finance, energy transition and resilience across its global network of country offices. Opportunities span staff positions, National Personnel Service Agreement posts and consultancies in more than 170 countries. Indian applicants can apply to both headquarters roles and the large India country-office portfolio.

Key responsibilities

  • Design and backstop national climate, nature and energy programmes
  • Prepare project documents, results frameworks and donor reports
  • Support climate finance and biodiversity finance initiatives
  • Advise country offices on resilience and energy transition policy

Eligibility & qualifications

Master's degree in environmental science, climate policy, energy, economics or a related field, with experience matching the specific consultancy, NPSA or staff post advertised.
