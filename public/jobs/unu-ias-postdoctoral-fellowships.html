Role overview

UNU-IAS in Tokyo advances sustainability research and education, hosting postdoctoral fellows, JSPS research fellows and visiting scholars working on biodiversity, climate, water and governance themes. Fellowships run for defined terms with stipends and research support in Japan. Calls are open internationally, and Indian researchers have strong representation in UNU-IAS programmes.

Key responsibilities

  • Conduct independent sustainability research with UNU-IAS faculty
  • Publish peer-reviewed and policy outputs
  • Participate in capacity-development programmes
  • Contribute to international research networks

Eligibility & qualifications

PhD completed (or near completion) in sustainability-related fields for postdoctoral fellows; specific JSPS and project fellowships list their own eligibility.
