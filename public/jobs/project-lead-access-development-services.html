Forestry, Wildlife & Afforestation

Project Lead

ACCESS Development Services · Sundargarh, Odisha

Full-Time Corporate / NGO Odisha Mid-Senior (3-6 Years)
Apply on in.indeed.com ↗ Official website ↗

Role overview

You will lead an integrated rural livelihoods programme in Sundargarh, focusing on natural resource management and climate-resilient agriculture for tribal households. The role involves managing project teams, overseeing watershed and irrigation interventions, and ensuring donor compliance. You will also build convergence with district administration to support community-led institutions and sustainable farming practices.

Key responsibilities

  • Lead project planning, implementation, and monitoring against established work plans and budgets.
  • Supervise and mentor field teams including agriculture, NRM, and MIS coordinators.
  • Provide technical guidance on watershed development, solar irrigation, and bio-input production.
  • Prepare high-quality donor reports and manage financial utilization according to organizational procedures.
  • Strengthen community institutions such as SHGs, farmer groups, and watershed committees.

Eligibility & qualifications

Bachelor's / Master's degree in the relevant discipline or equivalent experience
