Role overview

The Mittal Family Climate Fellowship supports postdoctoral scholars researching the impacts of climate change on South Asia. Topics include adaptation and resilience, mitigation, climate-related public health, water resource management, biodiversity, climate policy and governance, and climate technology solutions.

Key responsibilities

  • Conduct innovative research on climate change impacts on South Asia
  • Join the Mittal Institute community on Harvard's campus
  • May be invited to teach a Harvard class

Eligibility & qualifications

Postdoctoral scholars researching climate change impacts on South Asia.
