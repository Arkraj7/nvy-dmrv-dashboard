Role overview

Axiom is hiring 100+ GIS Trainees for a 6-month, day-shift engagement in Navi Mumbai. Walk-in interviews run 3-7 November 2026 at Rupa Solitaire, Mahape, Ghansoli. An entry point into GIS, mapping and geospatial technology for students and freshers, with on-the-job training and a monthly stipend.

Experience level: Entry Level / Fellowship

Qualification: Third-year B.A. (TYBA) students and graduates; interns and freshers welcome. Basic familiarity with GIS concepts is an advantage; training is provided on the job.

Key responsibilities include:

  • GIS digitisation, mapping and attribute data entry
  • Geospatial data cleaning, QA and basic analysis
  • Support to mapping and survey projects under senior GIS staff
  • Working with QGIS/ArcGIS for map production and updates
  • Maintaining spatial databases and project records
