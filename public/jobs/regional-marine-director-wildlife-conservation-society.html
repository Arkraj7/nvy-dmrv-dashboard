Role overview

Rwanda Organization: Wildlife Conservation Society Closing date: 23 Oct 2026 Reports to: Regional Technical Director, East Africa, Madagascar & West Indian Ocean Direct Reports: Africa Marine Mammals Lead Location: WCS Africa Regional Hub Office in Kigali, Rwanda, with frequent travel to sites.

Key responsibilities

  • Program Governance & Alignment: Lead regular strategic coordination meetings with regional leadership and country marine leads to ensure unified direction, operational coherence, and peer exchange
  • Where necessary, support refining organizational structures across national marine programs, aligning country-level teams, and optimizing the use of shared regional technical capacity
  • Fundraising: Represent WCS EAMWIO in interactions with multi- and bilateral donors
  • Work with country teams and global staff to co-create opportunities for regional projects, co-design them with country programs, and manage ongoing and future regional marine grants
  • Support country programs to develop single-country proposals

Eligibility & qualifications

Education: PhD or Master’s degree in Marine Biology, Fisheries Science, Conservation Biology, Marine Resource Management, or a related field. Experience: 7–10+ years of relevant work experience in marine conservation leadership, program administration, and multi-country project coordination, with preference for prior EAMWIO regional experience.
