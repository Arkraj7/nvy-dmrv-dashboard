Role overview

SGS India is hiring a technical ESG and sustainability professional for a techno-commercial / pre-sales role. The position translates client ESG requirements into technical solutions and supports proposals, client discussions, and business development across ESG advisory, BRSR, carbon footprint, net zero, and training.

Key responsibilities

  • Understand client ESG and sustainability requirements and propose technical solutions
  • Support pre-sales through client discussions, presentations, and technical proposals
  • Work with sales and technical teams on techno-commercial proposals
  • Support business development and conversion of ESG opportunities
  • Engage ESG, CSR, EHS, compliance, and procurement stakeholders

Eligibility & qualifications

3-6 years in ESG, sustainability, or environmental consulting, with a technical understanding of ESG frameworks, BRSR, GHG/carbon footprint, net zero, ESG assessments, and sustainability reporting.
