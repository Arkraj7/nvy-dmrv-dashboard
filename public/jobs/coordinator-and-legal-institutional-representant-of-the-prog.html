Role overview

The programme is closely supported by global staff: the head of programmes Latin America and Caribbean, a thematic advisor specialized in biodiversity, land governance and sustainable food systems, an M&E-advisor, a finance controller, and others. For our programme in Brazil, we are looking for an experienced, strategically minded and inspiring coordinator to take up this position on 1rst of December 2026 or by agreement: Coordinator and legal institutional representant of the programme in Brazil Your responsibilit

Key responsibilities

  • Assume the institutional legal representation of the HEKS/EPER Swiss branch in Brazil, via power of attorney
  • Lead the forward- thinking and creative strategic development of the programme in cooperation with the local and global teams and local actors within the financial framework
  • Ensure the effective and efficient implementation of the programme through participative monitoring, evaluation, learning and steering of the projects
  • Support the partners in drawing up annual and phase project-reports and write annual programme-reports, based on the reports of the partners
  • Support fundraising for the projects with Swiss donors by contributing to the drafting of projects and writing of reports and presenting them to the donors

Eligibility & qualifications

A relevant university degree and demonstrated professional experience in the field; see the official vacancy notice for exact requirements.
