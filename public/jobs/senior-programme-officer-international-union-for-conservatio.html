Role overview

Senior Programme Officer Vacancy #: 7725 Unit: Forest and Grasslands Organisation: International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN) Location: North America Regional Office, Washington D.C., United States of America Bonn, Germany, also considered as an alternative duty station.

Key responsibilities

  • Programmatic oversight and management
  • Manage assigned projects and workstreams on ecosystem restoration, drylands, rangelands, grasslands, forest landscapes and sustainable land management, ensuring effective delivery of agreed outputs and results
  • Provide technical guidance on the assessment, planning, implementation and monitoring of restoration and sustainable land management interventions, particularly in degraded dryland, grassland and rangeland ecosystems
  • Integrate considerations of soil health, vegetation dynamics, biodiversity, water resources, ecological connectivity, ecosystem functions and climate resilience into project planning and implementation
  • Support integrated approaches that address the drivers of land degradation and promote ecological restoration, sustainable land use and improved management of natural resources
