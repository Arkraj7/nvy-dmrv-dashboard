Role overview

TERI's doctoral programme invites fellows for research on climate policy instruments, carbon markets and sustainable development pathways. Fellows work within ongoing policy studies at the institute while registering for their PhD with TERI School of Advanced Studies. The programme blends quantitative policy analysis with field engagement across Indian states.

Key responsibilities

  • Develop a doctoral research plan aligned with ongoing climate policy studies
  • Conduct quantitative and qualitative policy analysis on assigned themes
  • Engage with stakeholders during field visits and consultations
  • Publish working papers and journal articles from the research
  • Present findings at internal seminars and external conferences

Eligibility & qualifications

Master's degree in Economics / Policy / Environment / Energy Studies (NET or equivalent fellowship preferred)

Climate and energy policy analysisQuantitative research methodsExcel and statistical toolsPolicy writingStakeholder engagement.

As per TERI doctoral fellowship norms (comparable to UGC-JRF)
