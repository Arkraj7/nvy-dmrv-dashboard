Generation Hope Goals 2027: Action for the SDGs

ESCAP · Bangkok, Multiple States / Remote, India

Fellowship / Internship Multiple States / Remote Entry Level / Fellowship
Apply on unescap.org ↗ Official website ↗

Role overview

Generation Hope Goals 2027 invites youth groups from the Asia-Pacific region to showcase innovative climate action solutions. Selected groups will participate in the 14th Asia-Pacific Forum on Sustainable Development in February 2027. Applicants must demonstrate an active project or service that contributes to specific Sustainable Development Goals.

Key responsibilities

  • Advance a climate action solution or service
  • Address SDGs 4, 10, 12, 15, or 17
  • Represent youth group at the 14th APFSD
  • Collaborate as a team of 3 to 5 members

Eligibility & qualifications

Groups of 3 to 5 members aged 14 to 24 years from the Asia-Pacific region.
