ESG, Carbon Markets & Corporate Sustainability

Associate, Power Utility Transformation Advisory

PwC India · Kolkata, West Bengal

Full-Time Corporate / NGO West Bengal Entry Level / Fellowship

Role overview

This role involves supporting the energy and power sector advisory team in executing renewable energy and utility transformation projects. You will work across functional teams to deliver operational strategies that assist clients in their transition to a low-carbon economy. The position requires strong analytical skills to manage data and produce high-quality reports for complex business environments.

Key responsibilities

  • Support the delivery of operational strategies within the renewable energy sector
  • Analyze power sector trends to shape client advisory and market success
  • Prepare professional reports, presentations, and client deliverables
  • Conduct data gathering and synthesize insights from complex utility datasets

Eligibility & qualifications

Bachelor's / Master's degree in the relevant discipline or equivalent experience
