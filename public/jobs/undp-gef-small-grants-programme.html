Role overview

The GEF Small Grants Programme, implemented by UNDP, provides financial and technical support to community organisations for climate, biodiversity and land projects in more than 125 countries, including a large India portfolio. National coordination teams issue annual calls for proposals that fund grassroots conservation, renewable energy and adaptation work. It is one of the most accessible international funding routes for Indian community organisations and field researchers.

Key responsibilities

  • Apply through the national SGP call for proposals
  • Deliver community-led biodiversity or climate projects
  • Maintain grant reporting and documentation standards
  • Engage local stakeholders and co-financing partners

Eligibility & qualifications

Open to civil-society organisations, community groups and indigenous organisations; grants up to USD 50,000 for community-led projects in biodiversity, climate change mitigation/adaptation, land degradation and chemicals management.
