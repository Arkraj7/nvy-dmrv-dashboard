Role overview

Consultant – Engagement of GIS Digitizers for Digitization of Rice Field Boundary Polygons at CIFOR-ICRAF (Landscape Alliance) is a verified Job / Career opportunity in GIS, Remote Sensing & Earth Observation, based in Sri Lanka. Applications close 31 October 2026. See the official listing for responsibilities, eligibility and how to apply.

Key responsibilities

  • Specific objectives are to: Digitize individual rice fields and plot boundaries from high-resolution satellite imagery, basemaps, or other reference datasets designated by ICRAF
  • Produce topologically correct and spatially accurate polygon datasets
  • Follow standardized digitization protocols and attribute structures provided by ICRAF
  • Maintain consistency among multiple GIS operators working simultaneously
  • Support rapid preparation of field-level spatial data required for integration with the AgriLook platform and related project applications
