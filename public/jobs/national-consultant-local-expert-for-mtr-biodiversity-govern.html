Role overview

Cabo Verde Organization: UN Development Programme Closing date: 5 Nov 2026 UNDP Cabo Verde is seeking a National Consultant/Local expert to support the Mid-Term Review (MTR) of the GEF-funded project “Strengthening Biodiversity Governance Systems for the Sustainable Management of Living Natural Resources in Cabo Verde”.

Key responsibilities

  • Deliver the role's core research and programme outputs.
  • Coordinate with internal teams, partners and external stakeholders.
  • Prepare high-quality written analysis, reports and briefings.
  • Support monitoring, reporting and delivery against agreed deadlines.
