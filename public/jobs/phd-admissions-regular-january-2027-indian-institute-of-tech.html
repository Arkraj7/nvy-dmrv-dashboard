Role overview

IIT Hyderabad invites applications for its full-time PhD program for the January 2027 session. The program covers diverse disciplines including Climate Change, Sustainability, Artificial Intelligence, and Engineering. Selected candidates will engage in advanced research under the guidance of expert faculty members across various departments.

Key responsibilities

  • Conduct cutting-edge research in chosen discipline
  • Collaborate with leading faculty members
  • Participate in departmental research programs
  • Adhere to academic and institutional guidelines

Eligibility & qualifications

Candidates must hold a Master's degree or equivalent in a relevant discipline as per department requirements.
