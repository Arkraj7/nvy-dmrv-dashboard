Role overview

This position supported ATREE's geospatial lab on applied research covering forest cover change, land-use transitions, and urban green infrastructure across Karnataka and the Western Ghats. The analyst processed satellite imagery and drone datasets, maintained spatial databases, and prepared cartographic outputs for peer-reviewed publications and state-level advisory reports.

Key responsibilities

  • Process and classify multispectral satellite imagery for forest cover and land-use change analysis
  • Build and maintain spatial databases and metadata standards for long-term research datasets
  • Prepare maps, figures, and spatial statistics for research publications and policy briefs
  • Support field teams with GPS data collection protocols and coordinate validation exercises
  • Contribute to urban heat island and green infrastructure assessments in Bengaluru
