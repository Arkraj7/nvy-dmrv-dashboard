Role overview

This role involves managing complex environmental, social, and sustainability consulting projects for industrial clients. You will oversee project execution, budgets, and technical quality while ensuring compliance with Indian regulations and international standards. The position requires frequent site visits across India to conduct audits, EHS assessments, and sustainability reporting tasks.

Experience level: Senior

Qualification: Bachelor's / Master's degree in the relevant discipline or equivalent experience

Key responsibilities include:

  • Plan and manage project execution, budgets, and resource allocation.
  • Execute environmental due diligence, EHS audits, and social risk assessments.
  • Coordinate BRSR reporting, GHG assurance, and climate risk projects.
  • Review technical reports to ensure accuracy and regulatory compliance.
  • Support business development through technical proposals and cost estimation.
