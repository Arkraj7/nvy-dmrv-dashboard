Role overview

Two-day online conference on 18-19 November 2026 covering GeoAI and machine learning, remote sensing and GIS, environmental monitoring, spatial analytics, coastal and water resources, urban and rural development and sustainability. Includes oral, poster and full-paper presentations plus a national map-making competition; selected manuscripts will be published with Elsevier.

Experience level: All levels

Qualification: Open to researchers, academicians, students, scientists and industry professionals.

Key responsibilities include:

  • Submit an abstract or full paper for presentation
  • Present research in oral or poster sessions
  • Enter the national-level map-making competition
  • Register and attend the online sessions
