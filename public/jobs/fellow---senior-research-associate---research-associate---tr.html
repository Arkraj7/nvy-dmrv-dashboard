Role overview

CPR is looking for researchers and interns to join the Transboundary Rivers, Ecologies & Development Studies (TREADS) initiative. Positions include Fellow, Senior Research Associate/Research Associate, and Full-Time Interns. Applications reviewed on a rolling basis.

Key responsibilities

  • Transboundary river research
  • Ecology and development studies
  • Policy analysis
  • Field research
  • Report writing

Eligibility & qualifications

Relevant degree in public policy, environmental studies, or related fields. Full-time, in-office roles based in New Delhi.
