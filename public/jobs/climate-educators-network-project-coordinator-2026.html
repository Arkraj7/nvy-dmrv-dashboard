Role overview

Climate Educators Network is hiring a Project Coordinator to lead Climate Action Forums across five partner schools in Kodathi, Bengaluru. The role coordinates school climate projects, teacher and student support, audits, campaigns, documentation, and event logistics, with regular travel to school sites.

Key responsibilities

  • Coordinate activities across five partner schools in Kodathi
  • Support teachers and students on climate projects, audits, and campaigns
  • Document impact stories, case studies, and social media content
  • Track progress, manage timelines, and handle event logistics
  • Travel regularly to partner school sites

Eligibility & qualifications

1-2 years in programme coordination, education, sustainability, or social impact; fluent spoken Kannada is mandatory; working knowledge of climate change and school education systems.
