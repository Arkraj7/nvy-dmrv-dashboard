Role overview

This role sits within the global supply chain sustainability team in Pune to drive decarbonization across transportation and warehousing networks. You will develop roadmaps to meet Path360 targets while managing emissions data and reporting for logistics operations. The position requires close collaboration with procurement and logistics teams to implement low-carbon transport solutions and improve overall operational efficiency.

Key responsibilities

  • Develop and execute a global logistics decarbonization roadmap.
  • Measure and report emissions across upstream and downstream transportation.
  • Identify carbon reduction opportunities in warehousing and distribution networks.
  • Engage with logistics service providers to adopt alternative fuels and EVs.
  • Create performance dashboards to track progress against sustainability targets.

Eligibility & qualifications
