Role overview

European Climate Pact Ambassadors commit to informing, inspiring and supporting climate policy and action in their communities and networks. Applications assessed on a rolling basis until 31 October 2026.

Experience level: Entry Level / Fellowship

Qualification: Anyone ready to inspire and motivate others to take climate action in their community or network.

Key responsibilities include:

  • Inform and inspire communities about climate action
  • Support climate policy locally
  • Share climate actions and spread the Pact's work
  • Engage neighbours, organisations and governments
