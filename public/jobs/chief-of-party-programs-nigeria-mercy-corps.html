Role overview

Mercy Corps works with communities to build economic opportunities, enhance water security, drive peace and good governance, and ensure food security. Our activities aim to build resilience through an integrated approach, helping communities and the most marginalized within them cope, adapt, and thrive in the context of numerous shocks and stresses.

Key responsibilities

  • Deliver the role's core research and programme outputs.
  • Coordinate with internal teams, partners and external stakeholders.
  • Prepare high-quality written analysis, reports and briefings.
  • Support monitoring, reporting and delivery against agreed deadlines.

Eligibility & qualifications

A relevant university degree and demonstrated professional experience in the field; see the official vacancy notice for exact requirements.
