Role overview

The World Bank Group is one of the largest financiers of climate and environment operations in developing countries, covering adaptation, carbon markets, blue economy and environmental safeguards. It recruits economists, engineers and environmental specialists across Washington DC and country offices. Indian nationals work across the institution, and the annual Young Professionals Program is open to candidates worldwide.

Experience level: Senior

Qualification: Advanced degree in economics, environmental science, engineering, finance or a related field with significant relevant experience; the Young Professionals Program has its own annual criteria.

Key responsibilities include:

  • Prepare climate and environment investment operations
  • Run analytical work on adaptation, carbon markets and safeguards
  • Supervise project implementation with client governments
  • Contribute to country climate diagnostics and lending strategy
