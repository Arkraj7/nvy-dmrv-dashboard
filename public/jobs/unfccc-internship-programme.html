Fellowship / Internship India Entry Level / Fellowship

Role overview

The UNFCCC internship programme places students and recent graduates in the secretariat's divisions in Bonn, working alongside negotiators, analysts and communications teams on the international climate process. Internships run for up to six months and are open to applicants of all nationalities. It is a recognised entry route into multilateral climate work for young Indian graduates.

Key responsibilities

  • Support negotiation sessions and inter-sessional work
  • Draft briefings, summaries and communication products
  • Maintain databases and knowledge platforms
  • Assist project teams across secretariat divisions

Eligibility & qualifications

Enrolled in or recently completed a bachelor's / master's degree in climate, environment, economics, law, communications or a related field; strong English writing skills.
