Role overview

The Gund Institute for Environment at the University of Vermont invites applications for its fully funded PhD program. This opportunity focuses on interdisciplinary research addressing global environmental challenges through rigorous academic inquiry. Successful candidates will join a collaborative community dedicated to sustainability, climate change, and biodiversity conservation while working alongside leading faculty members on high-impact projects.

Experience level: Entry Level / Fellowship

Qualification: Master's degree in the relevant discipline; doctoral registration as per institute / fellowship norms (UGC-NET / GATE preferred)

Key responsibilities include:

  • Conduct original interdisciplinary research on environmental sustainability
  • Collaborate with faculty mentors on specific climate or conservation projects
  • Participate in academic seminars and departmental workshops
  • Contribute to the institute's research publications and dissemination efforts
