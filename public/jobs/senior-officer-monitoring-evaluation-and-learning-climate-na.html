Role overview

Netherlands Organization: International Alert Closing date: 6 Nov 2026 This is an exciting opportunity to coordinate monitoring, evaluation and learning (MEL) activities across an impactful, international, multi-partner programme on water, peace and security. In addition, the successful candidate will also contribute to International Alert’s wider programming on climate and peace.

Experience level: Senior

Qualification: Occasional travel to our countries of operation. Essential requirements Knowledge of, and experience of applying to development or peacebuilding projects and programmes, monitoring and evaluation approaches and methodologies. Experience developing theories of change, indicators and logical frameworks for international development or peacebuilding projects and programmes. Unders

Key responsibilities include:

  • Monitoring and Evaluation Develop and disseminate common Alert M&E tools and resources, including drawing on global best practice to develop new resources when needed
  • Support country and global programmes to design programmes, develop realistic and useable theories of change and establish appropriate monitoring systems at both project and programme level
  • Convene the network of M&E leads across Alert’s programmes, to enable roll out of agreed organisational approaches to M&E, sharing of experiences, learning opportunities and peer support
  • Support capacity building of country programme Alert M&E staff by providing focussed trainings, inductions, and individual advice
  • Coordinate the annual reflection process with partners
