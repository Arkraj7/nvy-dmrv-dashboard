Fully Funded PhD Opportunities in Community Challenges

University of Nottingham · Nottingham, UK, United Kingdom

PhD / Doctoral Fellowship India Entry Level / Fellowship

Role overview

The University of Nottingham invites applications for its final round of fully funded PhD positions. These research roles focus on addressing specific community challenges through academic inquiry and practical engagement. Candidates will work alongside faculty to develop solutions that have a direct impact on local social and environmental sustainability within the Nottingham region.

Key responsibilities

  • Conduct original research on designated community challenges
  • Collaborate with local stakeholders and faculty supervisors
  • Develop evidence-based solutions for social and environmental issues
  • Produce high-quality academic publications and project reports

Eligibility & qualifications

Master's degree in the relevant discipline; doctoral registration as per institute / fellowship norms (UGC-NET / GATE preferred)

Compensation / fellowship

Fully funded

How to apply

Applications are submitted directly through the official employer portal. Urban Forest Park does not charge any fee and does not act as a recruitment intermediary.
