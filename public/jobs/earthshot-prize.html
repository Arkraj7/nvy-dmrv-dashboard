The Earthshot Prize - GBP 1 Million Awards for Environmental Solutions

The Earthshot Prize (Royal Foundation) · London, United Kingdom / Global, United Kingdom

Fellowship / Internship India Entry Level / Fellowship
Apply on earthshotprize.org ↗ Official website ↗

Role overview

The Earthshot Prize awards five one-million-pound prizes each year to the most inspiring and impactful environmental solutions, spanning climate, nature, oceans, air and waste. Finalists and winners receive global visibility and a fellowship of support, and entries from South Asia have featured strongly. Indian innovators working on clean air, restoration or circular economy solutions can be nominated or apply through the open process.

Key responsibilities

  • Submit a scalable environmental solution to the annual cycle
  • Evidence community and ecological impact
  • Prepare for the global awards process
  • Scale with fellowship support if shortlisted

Eligibility & qualifications

Open to innovators, communities, businesses and organisations worldwide with solutions across the five Earthshots: climate, biodiversity, nature protection, air and ocean.

Environmental innovationScalable business modelImpact evidenceStorytellingInternational partnerships.
