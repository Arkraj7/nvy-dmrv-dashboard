Business Development Manager – Renewable Energy & BESS

DEIF · Faridabad, Delhi NCR

Full-Time Corporate / NGO Delhi NCR Mid-Senior (3-6 Years)
Apply on linkedin.com ↗ Official website ↗

Role overview

This role focuses on expanding DEIF's market presence in renewable energy, battery energy storage, and microgrid sectors across India. You will identify new business opportunities and build strategic relationships with EPC contractors, developers, and utilities. The position requires regular travel to engage with clients and support the transition toward smarter, more sustainable power management systems.

Key responsibilities

  • Drive business development initiatives within the BESS and hybrid power sectors.
  • Build and maintain relationships with renewable energy developers and EPC contractors.
  • Promote intelligent control solutions for microgrids and renewable energy integration.
  • Lead commercial negotiations and contract discussions with strategic partners.
  • Provide market intelligence and customer feedback to the product management team.

Eligibility & qualifications

Bachelor's / Master's degree in the relevant discipline or equivalent experience
