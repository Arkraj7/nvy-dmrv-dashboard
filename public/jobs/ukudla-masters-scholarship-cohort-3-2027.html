Role overview

UKUDLA invites applications for fully funded master's scholarships at the University of Mpumalanga and the University of the Western Cape. Research areas include data science for food systems, the water-energy-food nexus, farming systems, agricultural economics, genomics, machine learning, and geospatial analysis.

Experience level: Entry Level / Fellowship

Qualification: African candidates with a four-year university degree equivalent to BSc Hons (NQF level 8) and at least a 68% average, a quantitative background in data science, computer science, bioinformatics, statistics, mathematics, or agricultural sciences, basic Python/R and machine learning skills, and eligibility to study in South Africa.

Key responsibilities include:

  • Complete a full-time master's programme at UMP or UWC from February 2027
  • Conduct research in sustainable food systems and applied data science
  • Participate in the UKUDLA Graduate Centre, summer schools, and short courses
  • Produce a master's thesis and contribute to UKUDLA research
