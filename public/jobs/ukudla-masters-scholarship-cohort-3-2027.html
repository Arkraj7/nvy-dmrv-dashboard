Master's Scholarships in Sustainable Food Systems and Applied Data Science (Cohort 3)

African German Centre for Sustainable and Resilient Food Systems (UKUDLA) · University of Mpumalanga / University of the Western Cape, South Africa

Fellowship / Internship India Entry Level / Fellowship
Apply on ukudla-coe.ac.za ↗ Official website ↗

Role overview

UKUDLA invites applications for fully funded master's scholarships at the University of Mpumalanga and the University of the Western Cape. Research areas include data science for food systems, the water-energy-food nexus, farming systems, agricultural economics, genomics, machine learning, and geospatial analysis.

Key responsibilities

  • Complete a full-time master's programme at UMP or UWC from February 2027
  • Conduct research in sustainable food systems and applied data science
  • Participate in the UKUDLA Graduate Centre, summer schools, and short courses
  • Produce a master's thesis and contribute to UKUDLA research

Eligibility & qualifications

African candidates with a four-year university degree equivalent to BSc Hons (NQF level 8) and at least a 68% average, a quantitative background in data science, computer science, bioinformatics, statistics, mathematics, or agricultural sciences, basic Python/R and machine learning skills, and eligibility to study in South Africa.

Data SciencePython or RMachine LearningFood SystemsGeospatial AnalysisStatistics.

Compensation / fellowship

DAAD-funded: EUR 650 monthly stipend + EUR 30 rent subsidy, tuition support up to EUR 3,000 per year, medical aid contribution up to EUR 20 per month, for up to 24 months

How to apply

Applications are submitted directly through the official employer portal. Urban Forest Park does not charge any fee and does not act as a recruitment intermediary.

Application deadline / window: 7 November 2026
