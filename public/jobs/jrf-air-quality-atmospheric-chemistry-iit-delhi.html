Role overview

A sponsored research project at IIT Delhi recruits a Junior Research Fellow to investigate urban air quality dynamics and secondary aerosol formation across the NCR. The fellow runs filter-based and real-time monitoring campaigns, curates datasets and applies statistical analysis to link emissions with pollution episodes. The role suits a candidate aiming for doctoral work in atmospheric chemistry.

Experience level: Entry Level / Fellowship

Qualification: M.Sc. / M.Tech. in Atmospheric Science / Environmental Engineering / Chemistry (UGC-NET or GATE qualified preferred)

Key responsibilities include:

  • Operate and maintain air-quality monitoring instruments during field campaigns
  • Process filter-based and real-time datasets and flag data-quality issues
  • Run chemical mass-balance and source-apportionment support analyses
  • Document protocols and instrument logs for the project repository
  • Contribute to progress reports and conference submissions
