Role overview

Gautam Buddha University invites applications for a Junior Research Fellow position to support ongoing scientific research projects. The selected candidate will work within the university laboratories to conduct experiments and data analysis under the guidance of principal investigators. This role offers an opportunity for early-career researchers to contribute to academic output while receiving a competitive monthly stipend.

Key responsibilities

  • Conduct laboratory experiments as per research protocols.
  • Collect and analyze scientific data for project reports.
  • Maintain detailed records of research activities and findings.
  • Assist faculty members in preparing academic publications.
  • Coordinate with team members for project-related field or lab work.

Eligibility & qualifications

M.Sc. in Life Sciences / Biotechnology / Environmental Science (NET/GATE preferred)

Laboratory research techniquesData analysisScientific writingBiotechnologyEnvironmental science
