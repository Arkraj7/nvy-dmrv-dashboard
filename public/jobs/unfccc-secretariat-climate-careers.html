Role overview

The UNFCCC secretariat in Bonn runs the international climate process: COP sessions, the Paris Agreement work programme and the transparency regime. It recruits policy, finance, adaptation, mitigation and data professionals on a rolling basis, and professional posts are open to all nationalities, including Indian candidates. Apply through the official UN careers system.

Key responsibilities

  • Support intergovernmental climate negotiations and work-programme delivery
  • Draft policy, finance or transparency analysis for Parties and the secretariat
  • Coordinate with UN agencies, observers and national focal points
  • Maintain data, reporting and knowledge products for the climate regime

Eligibility & qualifications

Advanced university degree (Master's or above) in climate, environment, economics, law, engineering or a related field, with professional experience as specified per vacancy.
