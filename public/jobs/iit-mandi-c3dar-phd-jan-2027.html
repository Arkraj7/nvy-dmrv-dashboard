Role overview

C3DAR at IIT Mandi invites applications for PhD, M.Tech (Research), and dual-degree programmes for the January-July 2027 semester. Research areas include climate change, hydrology, water resources, environmental engineering, cryospheric studies, forest fire dynamics, and disaster management.

Key responsibilities

  • Develop and run independent research in climate change or disaster management
  • Complete coursework and a doctoral or research thesis
  • Publish research and present at seminars
  • Engage with C3DAR faculty and interdisciplinary projects

Eligibility & qualifications

Master's or bachelor's degree in engineering, technology, sciences, or allied subjects with the prescribed CGPA/CPI, plus GATE/NET or an equivalent national examination unless exempt.

Climate ChangeHydrologyEnvironmental EngineeringDisaster ManagementResearch Methods.

Compensation / fellowship

Institute fellowship for regular full-time scholars; sponsored and self-sponsored modes available

How to apply

Applications are submitted directly through the official employer portal. Urban Forest Park does not charge any fee and does not act as a recruitment intermediary.
