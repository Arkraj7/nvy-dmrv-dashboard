Role overview

Naandi Water seeks a Quality Assurance Manager to oversee the QMS and compliance for their decentralized drinking water initiatives. Based in Hyderabad, you will lead quality audits, manage customer complaint resolution, and drive performance improvements across the iPure and iQuench brands. This role is essential for ensuring safe water access for underserved communities through rigorous operational standards and regulatory adherence.

Key responsibilities

  • Develop and improve the Quality Management System and internal SOPs.
  • Lead periodic reviews of drinking water safety guidelines.
  • Conduct internal and external quality audits to ensure regulatory compliance.
  • Manage customer complaints and perform root cause analysis for product recalls.
  • Prepare quality MIS reports and facilitate staff training programs.

Eligibility & qualifications

M.Sc. / B.Tech / M.Tech in Microbiology or related field
