Frequently Asked Questions

Which organisations hire GIS and remote sensing specialists in India?
ISRO / NRSC and regional remote sensing centres, state remote sensing application centres, IITs and central universities, environmental research institutes such as TERI and ATREE, urban development authorities and private geospatial consultancies all advertise geospatial roles — tracked daily by the registry.
What qualifications do GIS jobs require?
Most positions ask for an M.Sc. / M.Tech. in geoinformatics, remote sensing, geography or a related discipline, with hands-on skills in QGIS / ArcGIS, Google Earth Engine, Python or R and satellite-image processing. Project associate and young-professional roles may accept strong B.Tech. or fresh postgraduate candidates.
Are there urban heat island and climate-mapping roles in the registry?
Yes — urban heat-island modelling, land-surface temperature analysis and climate-mapping mandates with institutes and municipal programmes appear under this pathway as they are advertised.
Can freshers find geospatial jobs here?
Entry-level project associate, project assistant and fellowship positions in geospatial projects are tracked along with experienced roles. Use the experience filter in the live registry to see Entry Level / Fellowship openings.
