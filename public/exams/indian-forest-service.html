Indian Forest Service (IFS) Exam: Eligibility, Stages & Notification

The Indian Forest Service is one of the All India Services. Officers are recruited through the Civil Services Examination and serve in state forest departments after training.

In short

The Indian Forest Service is an All India Service recruited through the UPSC Civil Services Examination. A bachelor's degree in any discipline is required; selection runs prelims, mains, interview, medical, then training at IGNFA.

At a glance

Who runs the recruitment, where it is published and how applications are made.

Recruiting bodyUnion Public Service Commission (UPSC)
Service typeAll India Service, Group A
Where officers serveState forest departments, MoEFCC and attached institutions
Application modeOnline, through the UPSC portal
Official portalupsc.gov.in
CycleAnnual, listed in the UPSC examination calendar

Eligibility

The Civil Services Examination notification is the only authoritative source. These are the rules it has carried in recent cycles.

  • Degree: a bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university. Forestry is not a prerequisite at the application stage.
  • Age and attempts: the notification fixes the age band, the number of attempts and category relaxations each year. Read the current PDF rather than an old blog post.
  • Physical standards: the Indian Forest Service has its own medical and physical requirements, separate from the written examination.
  • Deadline discipline: eligibility is judged on the closing date printed in the notice, not on the exam date.

Selection stages

The sequence below is the standard route; a state or institute may drop or merge a stage.

StageWhat happens
Preliminary examinationTwo objective papers: General Studies and CSAT. CSAT is qualifying; the General Studies score decides who reaches the mains.
Main examinationWritten papers covering essay, general studies, an optional subject and the language papers.
Personality testInterview at UPSC, weighted alongside the mains.
Medical examinationIFS-specific physical and medical standards are applied after selection.
TrainingFoundation course followed by professional forestry training before posting.

What to study

The syllabus is published inside the examination notification. It is not reproduced here because it is long and the optional-subject list changes.

  • General studies papers cover history, polity, geography, economy, environment and current affairs.
  • One optional subject is chosen from the commission's list; forestry, botany, zoology and agriculture are common choices, not requirements.
  • Language papers test English and one Indian language at a qualifying standard.
  • Previous-year papers and the syllabus PDF are published on upsc.gov.in.

Pay

Pay is fixed by the 7th CPC matrix for the service and stated in the notification; allowances depend on the posting. No IFS salary figure is asserted on this page because the registry does not carry a current, verifiable one.

Where the notification appears

No cycle date is asserted on this page. These are the sources that publish it first.

  • UPSC examination calendar and the active examinations list on upsc.gov.in
  • The annual Civil Services Examination notification
  • Employment News / Rozgar Samachar, where the notification is also published
  • State forest department notices for deputation and specialist posts

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the IFS the same as a forest guard job?
No. The Indian Forest Service is a national gazetted service recruited by UPSC. Forest guard and forester posts are state-level recruitments with their own written tests and physical standards.
Can I apply with a non-forestry degree?
Yes. The examination is open to graduates of any discipline; forestry knowledge helps in the optional subject and interview, but it is not an application requirement.
When is the notification released?
UPSC publishes an annual calendar, but the exact notification date changes each year. Check the portal rather than relying on a date from a previous cycle.
