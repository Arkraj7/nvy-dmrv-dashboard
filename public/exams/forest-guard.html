Forest Guard Recruitment: Eligibility, Physical Test & Notification

Forest guard is the entry-level protection and field post in state forest departments. Every state runs its own recruitment, so eligibility and test pattern differ by state.

In short

Forest guard is a state-level field post recruited by state forest departments. Eligibility is usually Class 10 or 12 plus physical standards; selection is a written test and a physical efficiency test.

At a glance

Who runs the recruitment, where it is published and how applications are made.

Recruiting bodyState forest departments (for example MP Forest, Odisha Forest)
Service typeState field post, usually non-gazetted
Application modeState recruitment portal or district office, as notified
Official portalsmpforest.gov.in · forest.odisha.gov.in
CycleState-wise; some states recruit annually, others every few years
Core dutiesPatrol, fire watch, plantation and nursery work, wildlife protection support

Eligibility

There is no national rule. These are the criteria states publish most often; the state notification for the current cycle overrides everything here.

  • School qualification: usually Class 10 or Class 12. Some states accept either; a few ask for a forestry or agriculture certificate.
  • Age: commonly 18 to 30 with category and state relaxations. The exact band is in the notification.
  • Physical standards: height, chest and a running or walking test are standard. Measurements differ between states and between male and female candidates.
  • Local requirements: some states require domicile, local language proficiency or a specified district preference.
  • Documents: caste, domicile, education and physical-fitness certificates are checked before the appointment order.

Selection stages

The sequence below is the standard route; a state or institute may drop or merge a stage.

StageWhat happens
Written testObjective paper on general studies, environment and forestry awareness, arithmetic, reasoning and the state language.
Physical efficiency testRunning or walking to a fixed distance within a time limit, plus measurement checks.
Document verificationOriginal certificates, domicile and category proofs.
Medical examinationFitness for field duty, including vision and general health.

What to study

States publish their own syllabus with the notification. The pattern below is the common core.

  • General awareness with a state-specific section: geography, forests, wildlife and current schemes.
  • Basic arithmetic and reasoning at school-leaving level.
  • Language paper in the state's official language.
  • Physical preparation matters as much as the written paper: start running to the state's standard months before the test.

Pay

Forest guard pay follows the state pay matrix for the post, plus allowances. The figure is printed in the notification. There is no national salary for this post, and no number is asserted here.

Where the notification appears

No cycle date is asserted on this page. These are the sources that publish it first.

  • The state forest department recruitment page (for example mpforest.gov.in)
  • The state public service commission, when it conducts the recruitment
  • District forest office notices for local vacancies
  • Employment News and the state gazette

Frequently Asked Questions

Is forest guard a central government job?
No. Forest guard posts are recruited by state forest departments under state rules, so pay, eligibility and test pattern vary by state.
Can I prepare for the physical test in a few weeks?
Usually not. Most states set a running or walking standard that needs a few months of steady practice, so start well before the notification.
Do I need a forestry degree?
For forest guard, no. A school-leaving certificate is the usual minimum. Forestry qualifications matter more for ranger and range officer posts.
