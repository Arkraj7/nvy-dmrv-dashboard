CSIR JRF & Project Associate Recruitment: Eligibility & Selection

CSIR laboratories recruit research fellows through the national eligibility test and project staff through institute-level advertisements and walk-in interviews.

In short

CSIR recruits Junior Research Fellows through the CSIR-UGC NET and project associates through institute advertisements. A master's in a relevant science subject is required; stipends follow funding-agency norms.

Eligibility

Two routes exist and they are often confused: the national fellowship route and the institute project route. The advertisement for the specific post is authoritative.

  • For JRF: a master's degree in a relevant science subject plus a valid CSIR-UGC NET qualification, or an equivalent national test accepted by the institute.
  • For project associate: a master's degree with the percentage and discipline stated in that institute's advertisement; some posts accept a bachelor's degree.
  • Age: the CSIR-UGC NET information bulletin states the fellowship age limit and relaxations.
  • Registration: a valid PhD registration may be required for SRF-level positions.

Selection stages

The sequence below is the standard route; a state or institute may drop or merge a stage.

StageWhat happens
National eligibility testCSIR-UGC NET for the JRF route; some institutes accept GATE or a PhD entrance score.
Institute shortlistingApplications screened against the advertisement's discipline and percentage rules.
Interview or walk-inA technical interview, sometimes combined with a written screening test.
Document verificationDegree certificates, NET scorecard and category proofs.

What to study

The CSIR-UGC NET syllabus is subject-specific and published by NTA with the information bulletin.

  • Paper I covers aptitude, reasoning and research orientation.
  • Paper II covers the chosen science subject in depth.
  • For institute interviews, expect questions on your dissertation, lab techniques and the project's subject area.

Pay

Fellowship amounts follow the funding agency's norms and are printed in each advertisement; project associate pay is fixed by the institute's project rules. The registry reproduces the advertised figure for each post rather than a market average.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need CSIR-UGC NET for a project associate post?
Usually not. Project associate posts are institute advertisements and typically ask for a master's degree with a stated percentage. NET is required for the JRF fellowship route.
Is the CSIR JRF stipend taxable?
Fellowships are generally treated differently from salary for tax purposes, but the rules depend on the funding agency and your filing. Confirm with the institute and a tax adviser.
Can I hold a JRF without PhD registration?
Yes for the initial JRF period. Most institutes require registration for a PhD before upgrading to SRF.
