Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy constitutes the statutory notice issued by Urban Forest Park ("the Platform", "we", "us") describing the manner in which personal data is collected, processed, stored and disclosed in connection with the National Climate, Forestry, ESG & Geospatial Career Registry and the associated Nagar Van dMRV Dashboard and Green Calculator (collectively, "the Services"). This Policy is published in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

Notice: By accessing or using the Services, the Data Principal acknowledges having read and understood this Policy. Where consent is the basis of processing, such consent is free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, and may be withdrawn at any time in accordance with Section 5.0 of this Policy.

Section 1.0 Scope & Data Fiduciary Identity

1.1 This Policy applies to all personal data processed through the Platform's web properties, including the career registry at www.urbanforestpark.com, the career-pathway hubs, the Mandate Dispatch email briefing, the Telegram and WhatsApp alert channels referenced on the Platform, and the public research utilities operated by the Platform.

1.2 The Platform acts as the Data Fiduciary within the meaning of the DPDP Act in respect of the personal data described in Section 2.0. The Platform determines the purpose and means of processing such data.

1.3 The terms set out below carry the meanings assigned to them under the DPDP Act, read together with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules framed thereunder:

Table 1 — Statutory Definitions
TermMeaning
Data Principal The individual to whom the personal data relates — including candidates, researchers, subscribing professionals and visitors.
Data Fiduciary The entity that alone or in conjunction with other persons determines the purpose and means of processing personal data — for the Services, Urban Forest Park.
Personal Data Any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data, as defined under Section 2(t) of the DPDP Act.
Processing Any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, sharing, and erasure.
Consent The free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous agreement of the Data Principal, signified by a clear affirmative action, limited to the specified purpose.

Section 3.0 Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

3.1 The Platform sustains its open-access operations, in part, through advertising monetisation facilitated by Google AdSense. Google and its certified partners may serve advertisements on the Platform.

3.2 Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve advertisements to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

3.3 Where Google serves personalised advertising, it may use the DoubleClick DART cookie and related identifiers (including __gads, __gpi and IDE) to record advertisement interactions, apply frequency capping and measure aggregate campaign performance. Google's practices are described in its published policies at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3.4 Users may exercise control over personalised advertising through the following mechanisms:

  • Google Ads Settings — opt out of personalised advertising at https://adssettings.google.com;
  • Industry opt-out portals — www.aboutads.info and www.youronlinechoices.com;
  • This Platform's consent banner — selecting Essential Only records a preference restricting non-essential storage wherever technically implemented;
  • Browser controls — cookie storage may be limited or cleared through the settings of any modern browser.

3.5 Advertising cookies are not required for the functioning of the Services. Users who decline non-essential storage continue to access the registry without functional degradation. The Platform does not sell personal data to advertisers, and advertising partners receive no access to newsletter subscription lists or grievance correspondence.

Statutory Note

The disclosure in this Section 3.0 is published in fulfilment of the platform obligations of Google AdSense publishers and the notice requirements of the DPDP Act. A detailed specification of every cookie and local-storage key used on this domain is maintained in the Cookie Policy.

Section 4.0 External Job Portals & Third-Party Liability Disclaimer

4.1 The Services function exclusively as an informational career registry and research aggregator. Every job mandate published on the Platform links to the official application portal or advertisement of the parent institution, employer or recruitment authority.

4.2 Upon selecting an outbound link, the Data Principal transitions to a third-party website operating under its own terms of use, privacy policy and data-processing practices. The Platform exercises no control over, and assumes no responsibility for, the content, availability, security or data practices of any third-party destination.

4.3 The inclusion of any mandate, organisation or hyperlink does not constitute an endorsement, warranty or verification guarantee by the Platform of the terms advertised by the third party. Candidates are advised to independently verify eligibility, stipend, deadlines and institutional credentials at the authoritative source before applying.

4.4 The Platform does not receive, process or store applications, curricula vitae or supporting documents submitted to third-party portals. Any personal data disclosed in the course of an application is processed entirely by the receiving institution under its own privacy framework.

Section 5.0 Data Retention & Subscriber Opt-Out Rights

5.1 Personal data is retained only for so long as is necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, to comply with legal obligations, or to resolve disputes, after which it is erased or irreversibly anonymised.

  • Newsletter / dispatch subscriptions — retained until the subscriber withdraws consent (unsubscribes); erased within 30 days of the withdrawal request.
  • Server telemetry and security logs — retained for a maximum of 90 days, thereafter deleted or aggregated.
  • Analytics data — retained within Google Analytics property settings for a maximum of 14 months, after which it is automatically deleted.
  • Correspondence — retained for the period necessary to resolve the matter, ordinarily not exceeding 24 months.

5.2 Right to withdraw consent and opt out. The Data Principal may at any time withdraw consent to processing, including by:

  • using the unsubscribe link appended to every dispatch email;
  • using the Dispatch Subscription Preferences page; or
  • writing to the Grievance Officer at the contact set out in Section 6.0.

5.3 Withdrawal of consent operates prospectively and does not affect the lawfulness of processing undertaken prior to such withdrawal. Where processing is undertaken in reliance on legitimate uses or legal obligation, the Platform may continue such processing to the extent permitted by law.

Local Storage on Your Device

Dispatch records created by the subscription form are stored on the Data Principal's own device (ufp_newsletter) under the key described in the Cookie Policy. Clearing browser storage removes this record.

Section 6.0 DPDP Act, 2023 Compliance & Grievance Redressal

6.1 The Platform processes personal data in conformity with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, on the bases of consent and legitimate uses, and recognises the rights of Data Principals set out below:

  • Right to access information about personal data processed and the identities of parties with whom it has been shared;
  • Right to correction and erasure of personal data that is inaccurate, misleading, incomplete or no longer necessary;
  • Right of grievance redressal in respect of any act or omission of the Platform in relation to the data;
  • Right to nominate another individual to exercise the Data Principal's rights in the event of death or incapacity.

6.2 Requests to exercise these rights may be submitted to the Grievance Officer below and will be acknowledged and resolved within the timelines prescribed under the DPDP Act and rules thereunder.
