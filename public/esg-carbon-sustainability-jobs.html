Frequently Asked Questions

What ESG and sustainability roles are in demand in India?
ESG analyst and reporting roles, BRSR / sustainability disclosure specialists, carbon accounting and assurance, climate-risk modelling, climate finance and corporate sustainability manager positions are among the fastest-growing jobs — alongside environmental and social impact roles at development organisations.
What skills do carbon market jobs require?
Employers typically look for GHG accounting (GHG Protocol), familiarity with carbon-market mechanisms (India CCTS, voluntary markets), MRV, ISO 14064 / 14067 and sustainability-reporting standards, plus data and reporting tooling. Skill chips on each listing summarise the stated requirements.
Do these listings include BRSR, carbon accounting and CSR roles?
Yes — BRSR reporting, carbon accounting, climate disclosure and assurance jobs are tracked under this pathway. Corporate social responsibility jobs (CSR), environmental consulting and EHS sustainability roles appear when they carry a climate or ESG remit.
How does the registry verify corporate ESG vacancies?
Corporate roles are collected from official company career portals and verified job feeds, then normalised, de-duplicated and linked directly to the employer's application page. Closed jobs move to the Historical Archive for reference.
