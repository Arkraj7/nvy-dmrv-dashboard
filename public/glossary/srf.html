What is an SRF? Senior Research Fellow Explained

A Senior Research Fellow is the next rung after Junior Research Fellow. The upgrade usually requires PhD registration and a minimum period as JRF, and it carries a higher fellowship.

Live openings
34
Official source
CSIR, UGC and DST funding no
Updated
14 Nov 2026

Why it matters

This is where the term shows up in practice and in hiring.

  • SRF is not a separate exam; it follows from JRF performance and registration.
  • The fellowship amount is higher than JRF and is printed in the advertisement.
  • SRF positions are common in CSIR, ICAR and university projects.
  • After SRF, the usual next steps are research associate, project scientist or faculty.

The definition and rules above come from the body that owns the term. This site does not paraphrase a regulation into a simpler claim than the source supports.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long can I hold an SRF?
It is limited by the project duration and the funding agency's maximum fellowship period.
Is SRF permanent?
No. It is a fellowship tied to a project or PhD, renewed periodically.
Can I move from SRF to a permanent post?
Yes, through open recruitment for scientist or faculty positions.
