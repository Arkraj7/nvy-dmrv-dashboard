Terms

Definitions are kept short and point at the official source. Where a term has a legal definition, that definition wins.

BRSR (Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting)

BRSR is the disclosure framework introduced by SEBI for listed Indian companies. It replaced the earlier Business Responsibility Report (BRR) and asks companies to report environmental, social and governance information in a structured, comparable format.

ESG (Environmental, Social and Governance)

ESG stands for Environmental, Social and Governance. It is a way of evaluating a company beyond its financial statements, covering how it affects the environment, treats people and is run.

124 live listing(s) Read →

GHG Protocol (Greenhouse Gas Protocol)

The Greenhouse Gas Protocol is the most widely used standard for measuring and managing greenhouse-gas emissions. It was developed by the World Resources Institute and the World Business Council for Sustainable Development.

109 live listing(s) Read →

Carbon credit (Carbon credit / carbon market)

A carbon credit is a tradable certificate representing one tonne of carbon dioxide equivalent that has been reduced, avoided or removed from the atmosphere. Carbon markets let emitters buy credits to offset emissions they cannot yet eliminate.

229 live listing(s) Read →

Net zero (Net-zero emissions)

Net zero means reducing greenhouse-gas emissions to as close to zero as possible, with any remaining emissions balanced by removals from the atmosphere. It is a target for a company, city or country, not a single action.

229 live listing(s) Read →

SBTi (Science Based Targets initiative)

The Science Based Targets initiative is a partnership that helps companies set greenhouse-gas emission-reduction targets in line with the goals of the Paris Agreement. Targets are reviewed and validated against a published methodology.

131 live listing(s) Read →

dMRV (Digital Monitoring, Reporting and Verification)

dMRV stands for digital Monitoring, Reporting and Verification. It means using digital tools such as remote sensing, sensors and mobile applications to track the outcomes of a project, for example carbon sequestration or forest cover, in a way a third party can check.

197 live listing(s) Read →

Nagar Van (Nagar Van Yojana (urban forest))

Nagar Van, meaning 'city forest', is a scheme of the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change to create and manage urban forests in cities. The aim is to improve air quality, biodiversity, cooling and public access to green space.

74 live listing(s) Read →

CAMPA (Compensatory Afforestation Fund Management and Planning Authority)

CAMPA stands for the Compensatory Afforestation Fund Management and Planning Authority. It manages money collected when forest land is diverted for non-forest purposes such as roads or mining, and that money funds compensatory afforestation and related works.

64 live listing(s) Read →

JRF (Junior Research Fellow)

A Junior Research Fellow is a funded research position attached to a project or a PhD programme. The fellow does research, fieldwork and analysis under a principal investigator and receives a monthly fellowship rather than a salary.

31 live listing(s) Read →

SRF (Senior Research Fellow)

A Senior Research Fellow is the next rung after Junior Research Fellow. The upgrade usually requires PhD registration and a minimum period as JRF, and it carries a higher fellowship.

30 live listing(s) Read →

EIA (Environmental Impact Assessment)

Environmental Impact Assessment is the process of identifying and evaluating the likely environmental effects of a proposed project before a decision is made to approve it. In India it is governed by the EIA Notification, 2006, under the Environment (Protection) Act.

23 live listing(s) Read →

EHS (Environment, Health and Safety)

EHS stands for Environment, Health and Safety. It is the combined function in an organisation responsible for environmental compliance, worker safety and occupational health.

24 live listing(s) Read →

Circular economy (Circular economy)

A circular economy is an economic system aimed at eliminating waste and keeping materials in use for as long as possible through reuse, repair, refurbishment and recycling.

13 live listing(s) Read →

Greenwashing (Greenwashing)

Greenwashing is the practice of making misleading or unsubstantiated claims about the environmental benefits of a product, service or organisation. It ranges from vague language to selective disclosure of favourable data.

How to use this glossary

Read the definition, then check the live openings.

  • Match the term to the notice. If an advertisement uses BRSR, GHG Protocol or EIA, the relevant guide tells you what the employer is actually asking for.
  • Check the source. Every page links to the body that owns the term. Regulations change; the source is authoritative.
  • Follow the jobs. Each term page lists the live openings tagged with it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this glossary neutral?
Yes. It defines terms and links to sources. It does not rank employers or recommend products.
How often is it updated?
It is rebuilt with the site, and live-opening counts refresh from the registry on every build.
Can I suggest a term?
Yes, through the report page or by email to the registry.
