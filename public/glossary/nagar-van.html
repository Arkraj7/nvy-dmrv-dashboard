What is a Nagar Van? Urban Forests in India Explained

Nagar Van, meaning 'city forest', is a scheme of the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change to create and manage urban forests in cities. The aim is to improve air quality, biodiversity, cooling and public access to green space.

Why it matters

This is where the term shows up in practice and in hiring.

  • The scheme sets a target of around 400 Nagar Vans across Indian cities.
  • Sites are developed on forest or other available land within or near cities.
  • Urban Forest Park tracks these sites through its dMRV dashboard, finder and green calculator.
  • The work creates roles in urban forestry, GIS, carbon monitoring and community engagement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who funds a Nagar Van?
Funding comes through MoEFCC and the National Compensatory Afforestation Fund, with state forest departments implementing.
Can a citizen propose a site?
Urban Forest Park accepts site suggestions through its public survey, which are reviewed before appearing.
Is a Nagar Van the same as a city park?
Not exactly. It is a forested ecosystem with native species, not a landscaped garden.
