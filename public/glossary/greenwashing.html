What is Greenwashing? How to Spot It

Greenwashing is the practice of making misleading or unsubstantiated claims about the environmental benefits of a product, service or organisation. It ranges from vague language to selective disclosure of favourable data.

Why it matters

This is where the term shows up in practice and in hiring.

  • It erodes trust in genuine sustainability work and in ESG reporting.
  • Regulators in several jurisdictions are tightening rules against it, including in India through advertising and securities regulators.
  • ESG analysts and assurance professionals are hired partly to prevent it.
  • Job seekers should verify claims against published data and third-party assurance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is greenwashing illegal in India?
Misleading advertising is actionable under consumer protection law, and SEBI regulates ESG disclosures for listed companies.
How do I check a company's green claims?
Look for published data, defined metrics, third-party assurance and consistency across years.
Does greenwashing affect hiring?
Yes. Companies under scrutiny hire assurance and verification staff, which increases demand for rigorous ESG analysts.
