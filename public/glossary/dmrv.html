What is dMRV? Digital Monitoring, Reporting and Verification Explained

dMRV stands for digital Monitoring, Reporting and Verification. It means using digital tools such as remote sensing, sensors and mobile applications to track the outcomes of a project, for example carbon sequestration or forest cover, in a way a third party can check.

Why it matters

This is where the term shows up in practice and in hiring.

  • It reduces the cost and delay of manual field verification.
  • It makes project data auditable because inputs, assumptions and calculations stay visible.
  • It is increasingly required by carbon standards and funders for forest and nature projects.
  • Urban Forest Park's Nagar Van dashboard and green calculator are examples of dMRV-style tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is dMRV the same as MRV?
MRV is the broader process. dMRV specifically uses digital and remote-sensing methods to do it.
Does dMRV replace field visits?
No. It complements them. Field measurement remains the reference for many indicators.
Who uses dMRV?
Project developers, verifiers, funders and regulators, plus researchers and students studying urban forests.
