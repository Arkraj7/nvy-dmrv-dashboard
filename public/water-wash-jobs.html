What the sector involves

Water and WASH roles cover water supply, sanitation, treatment and resource management. Jal Jeevan Mission and urban water projects drive demand.

  • A degree in civil engineering, environmental engineering or water resources is standard.
  • Knowledge of water treatment, hydrology and sanitation systems is listed in most postings.
  • Field experience with water projects is valued.
  • Government agencies, NGOs and consultancies are the main employers.
  • Jal Jeevan Mission is creating thousands of water-related roles across rural India.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is WASH?
Water, Sanitation and Hygiene — a sector covering safe water supply, sanitation and hygiene promotion.
Is Jal Jeevan Mission hiring?
Yes, through state implementing agencies and partner organisations.
Do I need a civil engineering degree?
For engineering posts, yes. Science graduates apply to monitoring and programme roles.
Are water jobs well paid?
Government posts follow pay matrix rules. Private and NGO roles vary by organisation and location.
