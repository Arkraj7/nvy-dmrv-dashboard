What the work looks like

Roles in Delhi range from field ecology and forestry to environmental impact assessment, pollution monitoring, GIS and sustainability reporting. Field posts usually ask for a master's in environmental science, forestry, ecology or a related discipline; consulting and corporate roles add compliance, data and reporting skills.
