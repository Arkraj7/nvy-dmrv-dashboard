Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy specifies every cookie and local-storage key deployed on urbanforestpark.com and its subpages, the categories to which each belongs, the provider responsible, and the controls available to users. It supplements, and should be read together with, the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.

Section 1.0 Scope & Consent Model

1.1 This Policy applies to all storage mechanisms — HTTP cookies, and client-side localStorage / sessionStorage — deployed by the Platform on any page of urbanforestpark.com, whether set directly by the Platform or by third-party services engaged by it.

1.2 The Platform operates a three-tier consent model, presented to every first-time visitor through the Institutional Cookie & Telemetry Notice displayed at the foot of the viewport:

  • Strictly necessary storage — required for the interface to function (theme preference, consent record, email alert preferences). These are deployed without consent as they involve no tracking, in keeping with conventional interpretations of the DPDP Act and the IT Act, 2000.
  • Analytical & performance telemetry — Google Analytics cookies, deployed to measure aggregate traffic. Activated on Accept All Cookies.
  • Advertising & monetisation cookies — Google AdSense identifiers, deployed to serve relevant, non-intrusive advertisements. Activated on Accept All Cookies.

1.3 The consent record is stored locally under ufp_cookie_consent, with the corresponding decision timestamp under ufp_cookie_consent_ts. Use of the Essential Only control records a preference restricting non-essential storage wherever technically implemented.

Section 2.0 Cookie & Storage Specification

2.1 The following table constitutes the complete specification of storage mechanisms in use on this domain as of the Last Revised date. Storage keys are set in localStorage unless identified as a cookie.

Table 1 — Storage & Cookie Specification
Storage key / cookie Category Provider Purpose Duration
ufp_theme Strictly Necessary (localStorage) urbanforestpark.com Persists the user's Light / Night interface preference so pages render without flicker. Persistent — until cleared by the user.
ufp_cookie_consent · ufp_cookie_consent_ts Strictly Necessary (localStorage) urbanforestpark.com Records the cookie-consent decision and its timestamp, and powers the "Cookie Preferences" re-consent control in the global footer. Persistent — until cleared or revised by the user.
ufp_newsletter Strictly Necessary / Functional (localStorage) urbanforestpark.com Records the email-alert preferences entered in the subscription form (email, frequency, sector, timestamp) on the user's own device. Persistent — until cleared by the user.

_gid Analytical & Performance Telemetry Google LLC (Google Analytics) Distinguishes short-lived sessions for aggregate reporting. 24 hours.
__gads · __gpi Advertising & Monetisation Google LLC (Google AdSense) Frequency capping, aggregated advertising reporting and fraud prevention for advertisements served on this domain. Up to 13 months.
IDE Advertising & Monetisation Google LLC (DoubleClick / AdSense) Serves and measures advertisements, including re-targeting controls, on this and other domains participating in Google's advertising network. Up to 24 months.

2.2 Google's processing of advertising and analytics data is governed by its own policies, including the information published at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Users may opt out of personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com.

2.3 This specification is reviewed whenever new storage mechanisms are introduced. Where a new non-essential category is added, the consent banner is updated before deployment of the corresponding mechanism.

Section 3.0 Managing Your Preferences

3.1 Preferences may be set or revised at any time through the following controls:

  • On-site consent banner — on request, the Institutional Cookie & Telemetry Notice reopens via the Cookie Preferences control in the global footer present on every page. Selecting Accept All Cookies enables analytical and advertising storage; Essential Only restricts storage to strictly necessary keys.
  • Google Ads Settings — independent opt-out from personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com.
  • Industry portals — www.aboutads.info and www.youronlinechoices.com maintain network-wide preference registries.
  • Browser settings — all modern browsers permit blocking, restricting or clearing cookies and site data. Blocking strictly necessary storage may degrade interface preferences but does not prevent access to registry content.

Quick action: use the control to reopen the consent notice on this page and revise your current choice.

3.2 Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of storage undertaken prior to withdrawal. Records of the decision are retained as evidence of compliance.

Section 4.0 Policy Updates & Contact

4.1 This Policy may be revised to reflect changes in applicable law, the storage mechanisms in use, or the Platform's operations. Material revisions will be reflected in the "Last Revised" date within the Document Metadata box at the head of this page.

4.2 Questions, objections or grievances relating to cookies and tracking may be addressed to the Grievance & Data Protection Officer identified in the Privacy Policy, Section 6.0.
