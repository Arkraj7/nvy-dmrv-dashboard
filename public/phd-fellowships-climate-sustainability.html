Frequently Asked Questions

Where are PhD and postdoctoral positions in climate and sustainability advertised in India?
IITs, IISc, IIMs, central and state universities, TERI and TERI SAS, the Indian Institute of Forest Management, NRSC / ISRO centres and national research institutes advertise doctoral and postdoctoral openings on their official portals. The registry aggregates these advertisements daily and links to the source.
What funding supports PhD research in climate and sustainability in India?
Common funding routes include UGC-JRF, CSIR-JRF, DST-INSPIRE fellowships, institute assistantships and project-linked fellowships funded by DST, DBT, MoEFCC and international programmes. Individual listings state the fellowship amount and duration where advertised.
Can I find postdoctoral positions in this registry?
Yes — postdoctoral fellow and research associate (post-PhD) positions are tracked under this pathway and in the JRF / SRF & Project Associate hub. Postdoctoral mandates at IITs, IISc and national institutes are added as they are published.
How are PhD and fellowship listings verified?
Every listing is sourced from an official institute portal, a verified job feed or a reputable news announcement, then normalised and de-duplicated by the registry pipeline. Each dossier links straight to the official application page — the registry never charges fees.

Looking for something else?

The live registry carries every active mandate with state, sector and career-track filters — updated daily at 7 AM IST.
