How to apply with a thin CV

Most early-career applications fail on evidence, not enthusiasm.

  • One page, one direction. Lead with coursework, field work, tools and one concrete output per project.
  • Name your tools. QGIS, R, Python, Google Earth Engine, Excel, remote-sensing platforms - list what you can actually use.
  • Show a deliverable. A map, a report, a dataset or a field note is stronger than a claim of interest.
  • Match the notice. Mirror the qualification and experience wording from the official advertisement.
  • Check the mode. Walk-ins need documents and a reporting time; email applications need a single PDF.
