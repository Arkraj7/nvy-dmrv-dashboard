Global Opportunities — Frequently Asked Questions

What are the Global Opportunities on Urban Forest Park?
A curated international registry of climate, sustainability, forestry, energy, water, biodiversity and ESG opportunities published directly by official organisations — the UNFCCC, Green Climate Fund, UNDP, World Bank Group, IUCN, WRI, ESA, Future Earth, universities and major foundations. It covers jobs, fellowships, scholarships, grants, internships and competitions, and every entry links straight to the official application page — no fees.
How are India jobs and Global opportunities kept apart?
The two registries are strictly separated and never mixed. The jurisdiction switcher (India Jobs / Global Opportunities) swaps the whole dataset: India jobs are state-filtered and include JRF, SRF, PhD and government vacancies, while Global opportunities replace the state dropdown with a region filter (Global / Remote, Europe, North America, Asia-Pacific, Africa & Middle East, Latin America, UN / Multilateral). The global hub is backed by global_jobs.json, a file that never contains India records.
Which regions do the global opportunities cover?
Global / Remote, Europe, North America, Asia-Pacific, Africa & Middle East, Latin America and UN / Multilateral organisations. Remote and worldwide roles are grouped under Global / Remote, and UN bodies such as the UNFCCC, UNDP and Green Climate Fund are grouped under UN / Multilateral.
How do I receive new global opportunities as soon as they are published?
Install the UrbanForestPark app from the registry page and enable instant job notifications — new verified listings reach your device automatically. You can also follow the daily broadcast on Telegram (@urbanforestpark) and the weekly roundup on the Urban Forest Park LinkedIn page.
