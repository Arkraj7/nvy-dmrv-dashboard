Platform Governance & Methodology

Frequently Asked Questions

How the registry is governed, verified and maintained.

01 What is the mission of the Urban Forest Park Career Registry?

It aggregates verified environmental, forestry, geospatial and academic vacancies from official institutional notices into one searchable national registry. Every listing links to the original application source.

02 How are jobs and fellowships verified?

Each record passes domain verification, redirect unwrapping, duplicate hashing and editorial review. The dossier links to the official advertisement and the parent organisation’s website.

03 What happens to expired jobs?

Active jobs stay live for 30 days. Closed records move to the public Historical Archive, where researchers can compare stipends, skills and seasonal hiring patterns.

04 How often is the registry updated?

The pipeline runs daily at 07:00 IST. New jobs are published immediately. They are shared on Telegram and WhatsApp. The free email briefing is issued every 72 hours.

05 How does the registry relate to the dMRV Dashboard?

Urban Forest Park began as a dMRV and urban heat-island analytics initiative. It still maintains the open-access dMRV dashboard and urban forest Green Calculator alongside the career registry.

06 Is there any fee for candidates?

None. The registry, email briefing, Telegram channel and WhatsApp group are free for candidates. Urban Forest Park never charges a fee or acts as a recruitment intermediary.

07 What types of environmental jobs are listed?

The green jobs board lists environmental jobs across government forestry, JRF/SRF research, GIS and remote sensing, ESG and carbon, climate policy, renewable energy, waste management and EHS. It includes environmental internships, remote environmental jobs and global climate opportunities.

08 Are government environmental jobs included?

Yes. Government forestry, forest guard, forest ranger, wildlife biologist, pollution control board and state forest department roles are tracked alongside contractual research posts. Every listing links to the official notification.

09 Can freshers and interns use this green jobs board?

Yes. Environmental internships, entry-level project associate, JRF and GIS intern roles appear regularly. Use the experience filter to find entry-level environmental jobs and remote environmental jobs.

10 What is the difference between environmental jobs and ESG jobs?

Environmental jobs cover forestry, ecology, pollution control, EIA and field research. ESG jobs focus on corporate sustainability, BRSR reporting, carbon accounting, climate finance and climate risk.
