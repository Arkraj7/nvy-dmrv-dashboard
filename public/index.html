Platform Governance & Methodology

Frequently Asked Questions

How the registry is governed, verified and maintained.

01 What is the mission of the Urban Forest Park Career Registry?

It aggregates verified environmental, forestry, geospatial and academic vacancies from official institutional notices into one searchable national registry. Every listing links to the original application source.

02 How are jobs and fellowships verified?

Each record passes domain verification, redirect unwrapping, duplicate hashing and editorial review. The dossier links to the official advertisement and the parent organisation’s website.

03 What happens to expired jobs?

Active jobs stay live for 30 days. Closed records move to the public Historical Archive, where researchers can compare stipends, skills and seasonal hiring patterns.

04 How often is the registry updated?

The pipeline runs daily at 07:00 IST. New jobs are published immediately and shared on Telegram and WhatsApp; the free email briefing is issued every 72 hours.

05 How does the registry relate to the dMRV Dashboard?

Urban Forest Park began as a dMRV and urban heat-island analytics initiative. It still maintains the open-access dMRV dashboard and urban forest Green Calculator alongside the career registry.

06 Is there any fee for candidates?

None. The registry, email briefing, Telegram channel and WhatsApp group are free for candidates. Urban Forest Park never charges a fee or acts as a recruitment intermediary.
