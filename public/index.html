Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Urban Forest Park Career Registry?
It is India's national registry of active climate, forestry, environment, ESG, carbon-market, geospatial and academic research mandates. The registry tracks Junior Research Fellow (JRF), Senior Research Fellow (SRF), PhD, postdoctoral, project associate, young professional, faculty and corporate sustainability openings — publishing every listing with a direct link to the official application portal, with no fees and no intermediaries.
Which job categories and position types does the registry cover?
Six sectors — Academic / JRF / SRF / PhD positions, research associate and project staff (contractual), forestry, wildlife and afforestation, GIS, remote sensing and earth observation, ESG, carbon markets and corporate sustainability, and climate policy, think-tanks and fellowships — across seven position types at universities, IITs, IISc, government institutes, NGOs and companies.
How often are the job listings updated?
The registry refreshes every day at 7 AM IST. Automated collectors scan official portals, verified job feeds and news alerts; each mandate is normalised, de-duplicated and published, and expired mandates move to the Historical Archive.
How do I apply for a position listed here?
Open the position dossier and follow the ‘Apply on official portal’ link — every listing links straight to the employer's application page or advertisement. The registry never charges fees; always verify eligibility details in the official notification before applying.
