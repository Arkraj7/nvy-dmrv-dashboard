Platform Governance & Methodology

Frequently Asked Questions

A formal reference for candidates, institutions, researchers and partners on how this registry is governed, verified and maintained. Corresponding structured data is published as FAQPage schema in this page’s metadata.

01 What is the core mission of the Urban Forest Park Career Registry, and what gap does it resolve?

India’s environmental, forestry, geospatial and academic recruitment landscape is fragmented across hundreds of unindexed institutional notices — recruitment PDFs and advertisements issued by ICFRE institutes, the Wildlife Institute of India, IIFM, IIRS, ICAR bodies and Indian universities, alongside opportunities published separately by development think-tanks such as CEEW, WRI India, TERI and ATREE, and by corporate ESG portals. Urban Forest Park resolves this information asymmetry by aggregating, verifying and structuring these jobs and fellowships into a single searchable national registry, with every entry linked to a direct application source.

02 How are job listings, JRF/SRF fellowships, and institutional links verified on this platform?

Every listing passes through an automated verification pipeline before publication. Candidates are surfaced from employer portals, job platforms, verified feeds and institutional notices; each record then undergoes domain verification, redirect unwrapping (so links resolve to the publisher rather than a news wrapper), duplicate hashing and editorial structuring into a standardised dossier. Each dossier presents a direct application link and the verified website of the parent organisation, so applicants can confirm every detail at its authoritative source.

03 What happens to expired or closed jobs, and why is a historical archive maintained?

Active jobs remain in the registry for a 30-day lifecycle; when an application window closes, the record transitions automatically into the Historical Job Archive (archive.json). The archive is maintained as a public research asset: students, researchers and policy analysts can benchmark historical fellowship stipends, recurring technical requirements (GIS, Google Earth Engine, BRSR reporting, carbon markets) and seasonal hiring velocity across Indian states and union territories.

04 How frequently is the registry updated, and how do the Email, WhatsApp and Telegram job alerts work?

The registry is harvested by an automated pipeline every day at 07:00 AM IST. Verified jobs are published immediately and broadcast through instant Telegram and WhatsApp alert channels; a free email briefing of new jobs and fellowships is issued every 72 hours, summarising each period’s verified positions, registry telemetry and geographical coverage.

05 What is the relationship between the Career Registry and the Nagar Van Yojana dMRV Dashboard & Green Calculator?

Urban Forest Park originated as a digital Monitoring, Reporting and Verification (dMRV) and Urban Heat Island (UHI) analytics initiative for urban forestry in India. Alongside the national career registry, the platform continues to operate and maintain its open-access public research utilities — the Nagar Van dMRV Dashboard and the Green Calculator — freely available to institutions, researchers and citizens.

06 Is there any fee for candidates or institutions to access listings or subscribe to email alerts?

None. The registry and every alert channel — the website, the 72-hour email briefing, the Telegram channel and the WhatsApp group — are 100% open-access for candidates, researchers, academic scholars and institutional partners. Urban Forest Park does not charge candidates any fee at any stage and does not act as a recruitment intermediary; all applications are submitted through a direct application link.

Further statutory detail is published in the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Cookie Policy. Institutional enquiries: urbanforestpark@gmail.com.
