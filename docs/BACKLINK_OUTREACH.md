# Backlink Outreach Templates — Urban Forest Park

Use these templates to request backlinks from universities, NGOs, career offices and
environmental organisations. Personalise every email — generic blasts get ignored.

## Outreach tracking

Keep a simple spreadsheet with these columns:

| Organisation | Contact | Email sent | Date | Response | Link placed | Link URL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Aim for 5–10 personalised emails per week. Follow up once after 2 weeks.

## What NOT to do
- Do not buy links or use link farms.
- Do not send mass identical emails — personalise each one.
- Do not request links from irrelevant sites (SEO tools, gambling, etc.).
- Do not create fake social profiles to build links.
